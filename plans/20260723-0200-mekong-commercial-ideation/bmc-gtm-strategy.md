# Business Model Canvas — Mekong CLI Commercialization
> Date: 2026-07-23

## 1. Value Propositions
- **Primary:** "AI-powered operating system for one-person companies"
- **Secondary:** Plugin marketplace + governance layer for AI teams

## 2. Customer Segments
- **ICP:** Vietnamese tech founders, AI-native solo founders, small dev shops
- **JTBD:** "Run my business without hiring operations staff"

## 3. Channels
- **Vietnam:** Zalo OA, TikTok, ProductHunt VN, local dev forums
- **Global:** GitHub, ProductHunt, HackerNews, AI communities

## 4. Revenue Streams
- **Tier 1:** Free tier (limited commands)
- **Tier 2:** $29/month (full CLI, no marketplace)
- **Tier 3:** $99/month (marketplace + priority support)
- **Tier 4:** Enterprise (self-hosted, governance, SLA)

## 5. Cost Structure
- Fixed: infra, payments, legal/compliance
- Variable: MCU/API costs, support

## 6. Key Resources
- 319-command CLI engine
- Plugin marketplace + governance layer
- Payment infra (Polar checkout)

## 7. Key Activities
- Vietnam localization (Vietnamese, VN payments)
- Plugin marketplace launch
- Content marketing (Vietnamese + English)

## 8. Key Partnerships
- Polar (payments)
- VN payment gateways (VietQR, MoMo)
- Cloudflare (deploy infra)

## 9. Customer Relationships
- Self-serve + community (Discord/Zalo)
- High-touch onboarding for enterprise
