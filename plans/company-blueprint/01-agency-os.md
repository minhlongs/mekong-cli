# Step 01 — Agentic OS Design

## Business
Operate as a founder-led company with a narrow initial offer. The runtime is the product foundation; choose a single repeatable workflow for the first customer pilot. Avoid positioning an unvalidated agent catalog as a replacement for an entire human team.

## Agentic
| Function | Agent role | Human accountability |
|---|---|---|
| Founder | CEO agent: options, decisions, weekly review | Founder sets strategy and approves commitments |
| Product | PM agent: interview synthesis, requirements, prioritization | Founder validates problem and scope |
| Engineering | ENG agent: implementation, review preparation, reliability | Engineer approves production changes |
| Revenue | AE/CMO agents: research, content drafts, pipeline notes | Founder approves claims and outreach |
| Operations | OPS agent: runbooks, metrics, incident summaries | Owner handles incidents and customer impact |
| Governance | Risk agent: source checks, controls, audit preparation | Founder/counsel makes legal and risk decisions |

Use actual registered agents and available command surfaces; this table is an operating design, not a claim that these roles run autonomously today.

## Governance
Follow HARNESS.md, docs/core-contract.md, and docs/autonomy-model.md. Classify risk before tool execution, keep customer data minimal, log approvals, and require human review for high-impact actions. Start with human-in-the-loop automation and increase autonomy only after task-level evaluation.
