- 2 consecutive profitable years (or a waiver for tech companies under Law on Securities amendments).

## 5. Vietnam Entity Options

For the Vietnam operating subsidiary, two legal forms are available:

| Structure | Best For |
|---|---|
| **PTO (Private Technology Enterprise)** | Software / IT services, simpler setup, lower capital requirement |
| **DNNNT (Doanh Nghiep Noi Noi That -- Limited Liability Company)** | General business, standard corporate structure, easier to take on foreign investment |

Recommended: Start with a **PTO** (Cong ty Cong nghe thong tin) for the Vietnam operating entity, with the Singapore Pte Ltd as the holding company. This dual-structure setup is standard for Vietnamese tech startups targeting a future exit or listing.

## 7. Strategic Recommendations

1. **Incorporate before first paid customer.** Invoicing from a personal name creates accounting problems later.
2. **Separate IP into the holding entity.** All Mekong CLI copyrights, trademarks, and patents should sit in the Singapore Pte Ltd from day one, with a license back to the Vietnam operating entity.
3. **Build the cap table cleanly from the first equity grant.** Use a standard 409A valuation (or equivalent) and signed subscription agreements. Avoid verbal equity promises.
4. **Engage a corporate secretary** in Singapore from incorporation to maintain statutory records.
5. **Run a shadow ESG report** even at zero stage -- this becomes a requirement for institutional investors and exchange listings.
6. **Data protection compliance is not optional.** The Vietnam PDPD (Personal Data Protection Decree) applies to any entity processing personal data in Vietnam. Appoint a DPO, register data processing with the A05 department, and maintain a data inventory. Non-compliance can block licensing and listing.

## 8. Key Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Entity formation delayed past first revenue | Restated financials, legal cleanup | Incorporate before monetization |
| Cap table errors from early days | Dilution disputes, investor reluctance | Use cliff-vesting with signed documents |
| No audit trail for pre-revenue expenses | Cannot claim costs against future revenue | Track all expenses from today |
| PDPD non-compliance | Fines up to 5% of revenue, listing blocks | Register DPO from incorporation |
| Weak IP protection | Competitor copying, valuation hit | Trademark registration in VN + SG |
