# Step 02 — Governance and IPO Readiness

**Baseline:** pre-revenue planning posture; no listing or entity readiness is claimed. IPO readiness is a long-range control checklist, not a near-term milestone.

## Business
First establish the correct operating entity, ownership of intellectual property, accounting records, customer terms, and tax treatment with qualified counsel and accountants. Do not assume that a particular Singapore or Vietnam holding-company structure is optimal. Keep fundraising and IPO work behind validated demand and clean financial records.

## Agentic
Maintain an auditable record of material agent actions, tool permissions, approvals, model/provider use where available, and customer-data access. Agents can prepare checklists and draft policies; people verify legal interpretations and sign filings.

## Governance
As of 2026-10-14, official Vietnamese starting points include:
- Enterprise Law 59/2020/QH14 as amended by Law 76/2025/QH15, effective 2025-07-01.
- Enterprise registration Decree 168/2025/NĐ-CP, amended by Decree 296/2026/NĐ-CP.
- Personal Data Protection Law 91/2025/QH15 and implementing Decree 356/2025/NĐ-CP, both effective 2026-01-01.
- Artificial Intelligence Law 134/2025/QH15, effective 2026-03-01, and Cybersecurity Law 116/2025/QH15, effective 2026-07-01.
- E-invoice rules under Decree 123/2020/NĐ-CP as amended by Decree 70/2025/NĐ-CP, effective 2025-06-01, with Ministry of Finance Circular 32/2025/TT-BTC.

These are source pointers, not a complete applicability opinion. Map product data flows, AI functionality, entity location, customer location, employment, tax, licensing, and cross-border processing with counsel before launch. Recheck amendments before relying on this checklist.
