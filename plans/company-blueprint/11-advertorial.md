# Step 11 — Advertorial and Storytelling

## Business
Use a founder narrative grounded in the actual product: why local-first, provider-neutral, verifiable agent execution matters for a small operator. A case study may be written only after a real user completes a workflow and gives explicit publication consent.

Suggested narrative:
- The recurring task and its existing friction.
- The user’s workflow before Mekong CLI.
- A reproducible demonstration and human review step.
- What improved, what did not, and what remains manual.
- Evidence, date, scope, and next learning.

## Agentic
Agents can help structure interviews and draft anonymized stories. The human author checks the transcript against notes and gives the subject a factual review before publication.

## Governance
Never invent customer names, quotes, conversion results, savings, or case studies. Label founder experiments as founder experiments. Retain consent and attribution records; remove sensitive customer data.
