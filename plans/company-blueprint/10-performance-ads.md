## 2. Channel Strategy

### Twitter/X: Build-in-public thread ads
- **Format**: Long-form threads (10-15 tweets) showing real Mekong CLI sessions
- **Frequency**: 3x/week
- **Sample thread**: "I replaced 3 contractors with 1 CLI command. Here is the exact workflow."
- **Hashtags**: #buildinpublic #solopreneur #nocode #aiforstartups
- **CTAs**: Link in bio, DM for demo, first month free code
- **Assets**: Screen recordings of terminal sessions, side-by-side comparison GIFs

### Indie Hackers: Sponsored content
- **Format**: "How I Built X" case studies with revenue numbers
- **Frequency**: 1x/month deep dive + weekly comments on trending posts
- **Sample topic**: "From 0 to $5k MRR as a solo founder using AI agents"
- **Placement**: Sponsored posts ($200-500/ea), community replies
- **Conversion**: Direct link to product page, discount code for IH readers

### Google Ads: "AI for solo founders"
- **Target keywords**:
  - "AI for solo founders" (high intent, low competition)
  - "replace your team with AI" (problem-aware)
  - "one-person company tools" (niche segment)
  - "ai agents for startups" (broad but high-volume)
  - "build a startup alone" (aspirational)
  - "ai developer tools 2026" (research phase)
- **Ad copy variant A**: "Ship Production Code Solo. 22 AI departments. One CLI. $49/mo."
- **Ad copy variant B**: "Your AI Engineering Team. No hiring. No meetings. Just shipping."
- **Landing page**: /ads/solo-founder (dedicated landing, no nav distractions)
- **Budget**: $10/day test, scale winners to $50/day

### LinkedIn: "One-person company" thought leadership
- **Format**: Long-form posts (1200-1800 chars) on the future of solo entrepreneurship
- **Frequency**: 2x/week
- **Sample post**: "The 1-person SaaS company is no longer a dream. Here is the tech stack that makes it possible today."
- **Target audience**: Startup founders, indie devs, CTOs at small companies
- **Conversion**: Comment "CLI" for early access, DM campaigns
- **Carousel ads**: 5-slide deck showing productivity before/after Mekong CLI

## 3. Ad Creative Framework

### Structure (every ad follows this)
```
1. HOOK (1-2 sec / first line)
   - Grab attention: pain point or surprising stat
   - "Still managing a team?"

2. PROBLEM (2-3 sec / 2-3 lines)
   - Amplify the frustration
   - "10 SaaS tools, 50 meetings, zero output"

3. SOLUTION (3-5 sec / 3-5 lines)
   - Show the product as the obvious answer
   - "22 AI departments. One subscription."

4. SOCIAL PROOF (2-3 sec / optional)
   - "Used by 500+ solo founders"
   - Real testimonials or metrics

5. CTA (1-2 sec)
   - Clear, urgent, low-friction
   - "Install free -> $49/mo"
```

### Creative types by channel

| Channel | Creative Type | Format | Length |
|---------|--------------|--------|--------|
| Twitter/X | Text thread + GIF | 10-15 tweets | Long-form |
| Twitter/X | Short video | 30-60 sec | Vertical 9:16 |
| Indie Hackers | Case study (text) | 1500-2500 words | Blog post |
| Google Ads | Text only | 90 chars headline, 90 desc | Search ad |
| LinkedIn | Text post + image | 1200-1800 chars | Feed post |
| LinkedIn | Carousel | 5 slides | Document ad |
| LinkedIn | Video | 45-90 sec | Native video |

### Copywriting formulas

**AIDA (most common)**
- Attention: "Your 20-person team just became optional."
- Interest: "AI agents now handle engineering, marketing, sales, and support."
- Desire: "One CLI command deploys your entire product infra."
- Action: "Install. Configure. Ship. From $49/mo."

**PAS (problem-heavy)**
- Problem: "You spend 60% of your week in meetings."
- Agitate: "Every meeting costs you $200 in lost output. Your runway is burning."
- Solution: "22 AI departments that ship code. No meetings. No managers. No dilutive hires."

**FAB (feature-heavy for later stages)**
- Feature: "CLI auto-generates full-stack features from specs"
- Advantage: "Ship 5x faster than a traditional engineering team"
- Benefit: "Launch your SaaS in weeks, not months, for $49/mo"

## 4. Budget Phasing

### Zero Stage (Month 1-3): $0/mo
- **Channel**: Organic + content only
- **Activities**:
  - Twitter/X: Daily build-in-public threads
  - Indie Hackers: Weekly community participation (free)
  - LinkedIn: Organic posts 2x/week
  - SEO: Blog content targeting long-tail keywords
  - Product Hunt: Launch prep in Month 2
  - Community: Hacker News, Reddit r/SaaS, r/startups
- **Target**: 500 email signups, 100 active users
- **Success metric**: Signup-to-active conversion > 20%

### Test Stage (Month 4-6): $500/mo
- **Channel mix**:
  - Twitter/X ads: $150/mo (promoted threads + follower campaigns)
  - Indie Hackers sponsorship: $200/mo (1 sponsored post)
  - Google Ads: $100/mo (10 keywords, $3/day)
  - LinkedIn: $50/mo (boost top-performing organic post)
- **Target**: 2,000 signups, 400 active users
- **Success metric**: CAC < $10/signup
- **Winners**: Double down on channels with CAC < $10
- **Losers**: Cut channels with CAC > $30 after 60 days

### Scale Stage (Month 7-12): $2,000/mo
- **Channel mix**:
  - Twitter/X ads: $600/mo (retargeting + lookalike campaigns)
  - Indie Hackers sponsorship: $300/mo (2 sponsored posts)
  - Google Ads: $600/mo (expanded keywords, remarketing)
  - LinkedIn: $500/mo (sponsored content + InMail campaigns)
- **Target**: 10,000 signups, 3,000 active users
- **Success metric**: Blended CAC < $15, 30-day retention > 40%
- **Paid retargeting**: All channels retargeting website visitors

## 8. Creative Production Pipeline

```
Research (weekly, 2h)
  -> Trending hooks in solo founder / SaaS niches
  -> Competitor ad monitoring (X, LinkedIn, Google)
  -> Customer interview pull quotes

Draft (weekly, 3h)
  -> 5 new ad copies per channel
  -> 2 thread drafts for Twitter
  -> 1 case study outline for Indie Hackers
  -> 1 LinkedIn long-form draft

Design (weekly, 2h)
  -> Creative assets: screenshots, GIFs, charts
  -> Landing page variant updates
  -> Carousel / slide deck creation

Launch (daily, 30min)
  -> Schedule tweets, LinkedIn posts
  -> Monitor Google Ads performance
  -> Reply to Indie Hackers comments

Review (weekly, 1h)
  -> Channel performance dashboard
  -> Creative fatigue check (refresh CTR < 0.5%)
  -> Budget reallocation decisions
```

**Total weekly time investment**: 8.5 hours (zero-stage founder can maintain this solo).

## 9. Key Risks

| Risk | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|--------|------------|
| Creative fatigue (same hooks stop working) | CAC spikes 3x | Rotate pillar focus every 30 days |
| Google Ads low volume for niche terms | Not enough impressions | Broaden to adjacent keywords ("AI developer tools") |
| LinkedIn cost per click too high for seed stage | Burn budget fast | Cap at $50/mo until product-market fit proven |
| Twitter/X algorithm deprioritizes thread ads | Reach drops | Diversify into video + quote tweets |
| Indie Hackers audience is founder-heavy but shy on paid tools | Low conversion | Free tier / freemium hook for first 500 users |
