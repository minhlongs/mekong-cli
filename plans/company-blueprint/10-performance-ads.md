# Step 10 — Performance Ads and Creative Tests

## Business
Paid acquisition is not the first growth lever while ICP, offer, and conversion are unvalidated. Start with organic developer channels and direct research conversations. Prepare a small paid test only after a landing page, consented analytics, an offer, and a stop-loss budget exist.

Creative hypotheses to test:
- “Run agent workflows locally, with control over provider choice.”
- “Plan, execute, and verify repeatable work from the CLI.”
- “See the approval and audit boundaries before an agent acts.”

Use real product demos and clearly label limitations. Do not use unsupported salary savings, guaranteed output, unsupported department counts, or fake customer stories.

## Agentic
CMO agents can draft variants and summarize campaign data. Human review approves spend, audiences, claims, tracking, and external publication. OPS monitors spend and destinations.

## Governance
No ad budget is committed by this blueprint. Before a paid test, set a written cap, attribution window, stop condition, privacy review, and success measure. Keep a record of creative, source, spend, and results.
