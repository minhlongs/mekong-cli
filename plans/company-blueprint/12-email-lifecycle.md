# Step 12 — Email and Lifecycle Sequences

## Business
Define messages for future opt-in users:
- Welcome: installation and first-workflow guide.
- Activation: troubleshooting for the chosen workflow.
- Learning: optional examples that expand on verified product behavior.
- Pilot check-in: ask about outcomes and blockers.
- Renewal or cancellation: clear status, support, and data-retention instructions.

No sequence should imply that a free trial, paid plan, or billing flow currently exists unless it has been verified and launched.

## Agentic
Agents may draft content and classify support themes. Humans review every template, segment, and send rule. Do not let an agent initiate outbound campaigns without explicit approval.

## Governance
Use consent-based lists, minimal data, unsubscribe handling, retention limits, and a documented suppression process. Review email and direct-marketing rules with counsel for the actual countries and channels before launch.
