# Step 18 — Talent and Organization Design

## Business
Keep the organization founder-led while demand is unvalidated. Use contractors or hires only for a measured bottleneck: product reliability, customer support, security/privacy review, or a repeatable revenue channel. Define a job by outcome, cost, and decision authority before recruiting.

## Agentic
Human owners remain accountable for strategy, customer commitments, production changes, finances, and legal decisions. Agents support research, drafting, code review preparation, reporting, and routine reversible workflows. Assign a named human backup for every critical process.

## Governance
Document access, confidentiality, IP assignment, worker classification, and security onboarding with qualified advice for the relevant jurisdiction. Do not publish hiring thresholds or compensation forecasts without current evidence.
