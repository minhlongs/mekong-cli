# Talent + Org Design

## Current State: Zero-Human Workforce

| Dimension | Current |
|-----------|---------|
| Human employees | 0 |
| AI agents | 39 (entire workforce) |
| Human-to-AI ratio | 0:39 |
| Founder role | Sole human — strategy, capital, exceptions |
| Physical HQ | Binh Phap military camp (Vietnam) |
| Org structure | Binh Phap 5-factor military doctrine |

### Org Structure: Binh Phap Military Camp

The company operates as a military-style command hierarchy where AI agents fill every role:

- **Commander** (Founder): Strategic direction, capital allocation, go/no-go decisions
- **Agent Teams**: 39 specialized agents organized by function (sales, engineering, operations, finance, compliance, HR, legal, IR, ESG, growth, product)
- **Discipline**: Autonomous execution per SOP, human escalation for exceptions only
- **Agility**: No meetings, no HR overhead, no office politics — pure output

## Future State: Post-Revenue Scaling

### Hiring Triggers

| Trigger | Role | Headcount | Estimated Revenue |
|---------|------|-----------|-------------------|
| 100+ paying customers | Human support agent | 1 | $50k+/month |
| Content bottleneck  | Human marketing (content creation) | 1 | $100k+/month |
| Founder time constraint | Human operations / EA | 1 | $200k+/month |

### Workforce Ceiling

> Always keep < 3 humans. Leverage AI for everything else.

- Hard cap: 3 human employees total
- AI agents scale unlimited — 39 today, 100+ possible without adding a single human
- Humans handle only what AI cannot: nuanced customer empathy, high-stakes negotiation, brand voice, exceptions

### Role Profiles

#### 1. Human Support Agent (first hire)
- When: 100+ paying customers
- Responsibilities: Triage customer tickets, escalate to AI for technical depth, maintain satisfaction scores
- Tooling: Intercom + agent handoff to specialized AI agents
- Bilingual: Vietnamese + English (client requirement)

#### 2. Human Marketing / Content Creator (second hire)
- When: Content pipeline needs human editorial voice
- Responsibilities: Blog posts, social media, case studies, video scripts
- Tooling: AI drafts, human edits and approves
- Output: 10+ pieces/month, bilingual

#### 3. Human Operations / EA (third hire, optional)
- When: Founder cannot keep up with coordination
- Responsibilities: Schedule, vendor management, compliance tracking, agent oversight
- Tooling: Access to all agent dashboards, exception handling

## Design Principles

1. **Humans oversee, agents execute** — Every human hire is a multiplier, not a doer
2. **No middle management** — AI agents self-coordinate per SOP; humans handle strategic exceptions
3. **Bilingual by default** — All customer-facing humans must work in Vietnamese + English
4. **Remote-first** — Camp Binh Phap is HQ; humans work remotely with periodic on-site
5. **Asymmetric scaling** — 39 agents today, 200+ possible; humans stay under 3 always
