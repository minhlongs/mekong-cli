# Step 23 — ESG and Impact

## Business
Define impact claims narrowly and measure them before marketing. Potential measures include compute and hosting use per completed workflow, accessibility of local-first deployment, and user-reported time spent on the selected task. Do not claim jobs saved or emissions avoided without a transparent baseline and method.

## Agentic
OPS gathers operational data with minimal collection. Agents can summarize trends; Engineering verifies measurement quality and Governance reviews methodology.

## Governance
Use a documented boundary, period, source, and calculation method for each metric. Protect user and employee data. Revisit relevant reporting expectations when company size, customers, contracts, or financing requirements change.
