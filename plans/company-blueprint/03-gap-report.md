# Step 03 — Gap Report and Six-Month Roadmap

## Business
| Gap | Why it matters | First response |
|---|---|---|
| No validated ICP or paid demand evidence | Product scope and revenue path remain uncertain | Conduct problem interviews and run a small design-partner pilot |
| No verified pricing or unit economics | $1M ARR cannot yet be forecast | Test willingness to pay; measure model, support, and infrastructure costs |
| No documented activation and retention baseline | Cannot tell whether users receive recurring value | Instrument the selected workflow with consent and data minimization |
| Unclear launch and support offer | Adoption may fail even if runtime works | Write install, first-value, support, and escalation paths |
| Legal applicability not assessed for actual flows | Data, AI, tax, and entity obligations depend on facts | Map data and service flows; obtain professional review |

## Agentic
Use the founder and PM roles for interviews and synthesis; Engineering instruments one workflow; Ops owns runbooks and metrics; Governance tracks claims, approvals, and sources. Agent output is a draft until reviewed by the accountable owner.

## Governance
**Months 1–2:** Verify current product facts, select one ICP/workflow, conduct interviews, document evidence and objections.
**Month 3:** Run a bounded pilot with clear consent, success criteria, support route, and stop conditions.
**Months 4–6:** If evidence supports it, test one paid offer, measure activation/repeat use/cost to serve, and prepare privacy, AI, tax, and invoice controls with qualified advisors.
**Months 7–12:** Scale only if paid use, retention, delivery cost, and acquisition repeatability support it. Review progress toward $1M ARR as a target, not a promised outcome.
