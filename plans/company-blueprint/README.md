# Mekong CLI — Company Blueprint

**Baseline checked:** 2026-10-14
**Stage:** Zero→PSF, pending reconfirmation of current customer evidence
**Business:** Open-source, local-first AI agent runtime for solo companies
**Goal:** $1M ARR within 12 months, aspirational and not a forecast

This blueprint has been refreshed around verified repository facts and explicit hypotheses. It does not claim paying customers, validated pricing, a completed beta, proven unit economics, or a verified global TAM. The prior documents contained inconsistent license, product-size, pricing, customer, and market claims; use this version as the current planning baseline.

## Revenue and evidence rules

1. Confirm the ICP and recurring job before expanding product scope.
2. Test one workflow and one offer with consented design partners.
3. Measure actual payments, repeat use, support effort, and delivery cost before forecasting.
4. Treat the $1M ARR target as an aspiration until observed retention, price, and acquisition support it.
5. Recheck legal and market sources before external use.
