# Step 04 — Refactor to the 2026 Planning Frame

## Business
Organize the operating plan around outcomes rather than catalog size:
1. One ICP and recurring job.
2. One workflow that produces a measurable outcome.
3. A repeatable install, onboarding, support, and payment path if demand is proven.
4. Evidence-based expansion into adjacent workflows.

Keep Mekong CLI runtime capabilities distinct from separate MekongMind.com funnels until customer research confirms a shared buyer and purchase.

## Agentic
Map each outcome to a human owner, agent support role, permitted tools, required context, verification step, and escalation route. Reuse the lifecycle documented in the core contract; do not create a parallel lifecycle in planning material.

## Governance
Use dated evidence, explicit assumptions, acceptance criteria, and a named decision owner in each plan. Align the tri-layer blueprint with HARNESS.md, docs/core-contract.md, and docs/autonomy-model.md. Correct stale claims in the existing blueprint before reusing it externally.
