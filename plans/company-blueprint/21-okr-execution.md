# Step 21 — Agentic Execution and OKRs

## Business
**12-month objective:** establish a validated, repeatable business path toward the aspirational $1M ARR target.

- **Months 1–3:** validate one ICP and one recurring workflow; complete 15 interviews, recruit up to three design partners, and publish a decision memo with evidence and counter-evidence.
- **Months 4–6:** if pilots show repeat value, test one paid offer; record real payments, activation, repeat use, support effort, and delivery costs.
- **Months 7–9:** improve retention and delivery economics; continue only channels that produce qualified users.
- **Months 10–12:** assess repeatability and progress toward the ARR aspiration; revise the target if observed price, retention, capacity, or acquisition do not support it.

These are planned milestones, not claims about existing users or results.

## Agentic
CEO coordinates the weekly review; PM owns learning milestones; ENG owns reliability and measurement; Revenue owns approved experiments; OPS owns support and incidents; Governance owns evidence and approval records.

## Governance
For each KR, record an owner, baseline, evidence source, review date, and status. A missed KR triggers diagnosis and a decision, not fabricated completion. Reassess stage on evidence rather than calendar date.
