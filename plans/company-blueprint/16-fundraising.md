# Fundraising + VC Narrative

## Fundraising Strategy Overview

Mekong CLI is an AI-native venture studio operating system. The fundraise thesis is simple: replace $500K/year in human team salary with a $49/month AI agent workforce. Twenty-two autonomous departments, one human operator.

| Use of Funds | Product development (70%), GTM (20%), Legal & Operations (10%) |

## Traction Required Before Fundraise

| Metric | Target | Rationale |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| Paying Customers | 100 | Validates willingness to pay at $49/mo |
| Monthly Recurring Revenue | $5K MRR | Proves unit economics at scale |
| 3-Month Retention Data | >80% gross retention | Confirms product-market fit stickiness |
| Customer Acquisition Cost | < $200 | Enables profitable growth with organic + paid |
| Lifetime Value | > $1,000 | 5x+ LTV/CAC ratio for VC-grade unit economics |
| Monthly Active Users | > 300 MAU | Shows engagement beyond initial signup |

### Traction Validation Strategy

- **Phase 1 (Month 1-2):** Launch early adopter program -- 50 founders get 3 months free in exchange for structured feedback and case studies. Target: 20 active users, $0 MRR.
- **Phase 2 (Month 3-4):** Open paid tier at $49/mo. Target: 50 paying customers, $2.5K MRR. Begin retention data collection.
- **Phase 3 (Month 5-6):** Scale GTM with content marketing and affiliate partnerships. Target: 100 paying customers, $5K MRR, 3-month retention window.

## Pitch Deck Structure (10 Slides)

1. **Problem** -- Startups waste $500K+ on team salary before finding product-market fit. Hiring is slow, expensive, and high-risk.
2. **Solution** -- Twenty-two autonomous AI departments. One human operator. $49/month. No hiring, no firing, no HR overhead.
3. **Product** -- Demo video: Fundraising department drafts pitch decks, Engineering department ships code, Marketing runs campaigns. All from a single CLI.
4. **Market Size** -- TAM: 10M+ early-stage startups globally. SAM: 500K AI-native founders. Target: $5B ARR at scale.
5. **Traction** -- X paying customers, $Y MRR, Z% monthly growth. Key case studies and testimonials.
6. **Business Model** -- $49/month flat SaaS. No per-agent pricing. Unlimited departments. Low chark (price insensitive, value dense).
7. **Competition** -- Traditional BPO ($100K+/yr) vs. partial AI tools (single-agent Copilot) vs. full-stack agent platform (Mekong).
8. **Go-to-Market** -- Developer community (Hacker News, GitHub, X/Twitter). Product-led growth with CLI-first onboarding. Affiliate program for agency partners.
9. **Team** -- Founder-market fit. Ship velocity. AI systems experience.
10. **Ask** -- $500K pre-seed. 12 months runway. Key milestones: 1K paying customers, $50K MRR, enterprise tier launch.

## Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| AI quality inconsistent across departments | Continuous fine-tuning with customer feedback loops; confidence scoring before execution |
| Market education required (founders don't trust AI agents) | Case study library, transparency dashboards, refund guarantee for first month |
| Single point of failure (founder-dependent) | Build repeatable agent training pipeline; hire ops lead post-seed |
| LLM cost out of control | Per-call cost tracking, model routing (cheap models for simple tasks), caching layer |
