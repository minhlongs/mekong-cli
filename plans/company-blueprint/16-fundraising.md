# Step 16 — Fundraising and Investor Narrative

## Business
Fundraising is optional and premature until the problem, user, product outcome, and monetization path have evidence. A future narrative can explain:
- The recurring problem and target user.
- The selected workflow and what the runtime does.
- What is open source and what a potential paid offer includes.
- Demonstrated use, retention, revenue, costs, and risks.
- Milestones and the use of funds, if fundraising is chosen.

The $1M ARR in 12 months is an internal aspiration, not a forecast or investor claim. Do not include an unsupported funding ask or implied customer traction.

## Agentic
Agents can prepare a source-linked draft deck, financial model structure, and diligence checklist. The founder verifies every number and approves investor communications.

## Governance
Use actuals, cohort definitions, and dated source documents. Reconcile any revenue with invoices, refunds, and bank/payment records. Do not disclose customer data without permission. Keep cap-table, IP, and entity statements subject to legal review.
