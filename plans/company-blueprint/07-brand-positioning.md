# Step 07 — Brand Positioning

## Business
**Positioning hypothesis:** Mekong CLI helps solo operators run repeatable work through an open-source, local-first AI agent runtime with explicit verification and governance boundaries.

**Proof points available to substantiate carefully:** public source repository, MIT license stated in the repository, and the documented runtime lifecycle and governance contracts. These prove product and design choices, not customer outcomes.

**Differentiation to test:** local control, provider choice, composable CLI workflows, and an auditable path from goal to verified result. Avoid “replaces 50 employees,” “runs your whole company,” “first,” or “best” until independently substantiated.

## Agentic
Agents may draft messaging variants from approved product facts. Human review is required for comparative, performance, savings, security, and customer-result claims.

## Governance
Keep a claims ledger with statement, evidence, date, scope, and owner. Distinguish shipped functionality from roadmap and test results from production customer outcomes. Use MIT consistently with the repository license.
