# Step 22 — Board Governance

## Business
Before a formal board exists, run a monthly founder review covering customer evidence, product reliability, actual cash and costs, risks, and decisions. Invite an advisor only for a defined expertise gap. Do not claim independent directors, committees, or board oversight unless formally established.

## Agentic
Agents prepare a monthly pack from approved metrics and decision logs. The founder checks source data, records corrections, and owns decisions. No agent votes, signs, or approves a legal or financing commitment.

## Governance
Maintain a decision register with date, options, evidence, owner, approval, and follow-up. If the company incorporates or raises capital, have counsel establish the appropriate board, shareholder, reporting, conflict, and recordkeeping requirements.
