# 22. Board Governance

**Owner:** Founder/CEO
**Status:** Not started
**Priority:** Medium (pre-seed / post-seed transition)

---

## Current State

- **Zero board structure.** Founder is the sole decision maker for all strategic, financial, and operational matters.
- No formal governance documents exist beyond the founding incorporation paperwork.
- No regular cadence for strategic reviews or investor reporting.
- All decisions are made ad-hoc via direct founder judgment.

---

## Future Board Structure (Post-Seed)

Once a seed round closes, the board will formalise into a three-person body:

| Seat | Role | Appointed By |
|------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Founder / CEO | Founder |
| 2 | Investor representative | Lead seed investor |
| 3 | Independent advisor | Mutually agreed by Founder + Investor |

**Key principles:**

- The independent advisor brings domain or operational expertise not held by the other two members.
- Board composition avoids deadlock: the Founder retains tie-breaking authority for ordinary-course decisions; major decisions (sale, dissolution, dilution > 20 %) require a supermajority.
- Observer seats may be granted to key strategic partners without voting rights.
- Board members serve one-year renewable terms.

---

## Meeting Cadence

| Frequency | Type | Duration | Purpose |
|-----------|------|----------|---------|
| Monthly | Regular board meeting | 60 min | Operational review, KPI tracking, strategic decisions |
| Quarterly | Deep-dive session | 120 min | Financial review, product roadmap, fundraising update |
| Annual | Strategy offsite | Half-day | Annual planning, board self-assessment, governance review |

**Monthly meeting structure:**

1. Call to order and quorum check (2 min)
2. Approval of previous minutes (3 min)
3. CEO report / strategic update (15 min)
4. Financial review (10 min)
5. Operating metrics and KPIs (10 min)
6. Key decisions and voting items (10 min)
7. Independent advisor observations (5 min)
8. Action items and next meeting date (5 min)

## Governance Documents Required

| Document | Purpose | Owner | Deadline |
|----------|---------|-------|----------|
| Shareholder agreement | Defines rights, obligations, transfer restrictions, drag-along, tag-along, pre-emptive rights | Legal counsel | Before seed close |
| Board resolutions template | Standardised format for formal board decisions, including written consent in lieu of meetings | Founder / Corp Sec | Before first board meeting |
| Financial reporting template | Monthly/quarterly P&L, cash flow, burn rate, ARR/MRR, unit economics | Finance lead | Before first board meeting |
| Code of conduct | Director ethics, conflict-of-interest disclosure, confidentiality obligations | Founder | Within 30 days of board formation |
| Board calendar | 12-month forward schedule of all meetings, reporting deadlines, and decision milestones | Founder / Corp Sec | At board formation |

---

## Financial Reporting Package

The financial reporting template sent to board members 72 hours before each meeting must include:

- **Income statement** (actual vs budget vs prior period)
- **Cash flow statement** with runway analysis
- **Balance sheet** (monthly snapshot)
- **ARR / MRR breakdown** by customer cohort
- **Burn multiple** (net burn / net new ARR)
- **Unit economics** (CAC, LTV, payback period)
- **Capex and material commitments**
- **Headcount cost summary**

## Decision Rights Matrix

| Decision Type | Founder | Board | Shareholders |
|---------------|---------|-------|-------------|
| Operating budget within plan | Approve | Inform | — |
| Hiring VP+ / C-suite | Propose | Approve | — |
| Equity issuance > 5 % | Propose | Approve | Approve |
| M&A / acquisition | Propose | Approve | Approve |
| Dissolution / winding up | Propose | Approve | Approve |
| Debt > 3 months' burn | Propose | Approve | Inform |
| Change of business model | Propose | Approve | Inform |
