# Step 14 — GTM Experiments and Channel Testing

## Business
Run sequential, low-cost experiments:
| Experiment | Hypothesis | Evidence to capture | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Founder interviews | A narrow user segment has a recurring costly job | Recent behavior, current workaround, urgency | Keep, refine, or reject ICP |
| Workflow demo | One runtime workflow produces clear value | Completion, errors, time, user assessment | Improve or narrow workflow |
| Design-partner pilot | Users will return to the workflow | Repeat use, blockers, support effort | Continue or stop pilot |
| Offer test | A clear offer can convert to payment | Written willingness to pay and actual payments | Keep or revise offer |
| Channel test | One channel reaches qualified users | Qualified conversations per time/cash spent | Continue only with evidence |

## Agentic
Agents can gather public information, draft experiment materials, and summarize outcomes. A human approves outreach, spend, and any claim.

## Governance
Pre-register hypothesis, sample, evidence, stop rule, and decision owner. Avoid overclaiming from small samples. Do not launch paid acquisition until privacy, instrumentation, landing copy, and budget controls are ready.
