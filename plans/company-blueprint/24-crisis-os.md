# Step 24 — Crisis and Reputation OS

## Business
Prepare response paths for: security or privacy incident, harmful or incorrect agent action, service/provider outage, billing dispute, license/IP concern, and public claim error. Prioritize containment, user impact, recovery, and clear correction over defensive messaging.

## Agentic
OPS detects and records incidents; Engineering contains and restores service; Governance assesses privacy, legal, and notification questions; the founder approves public statements and customer communications. Agents may draft incident summaries but do not send them.

## Governance
Maintain named contacts, escalation thresholds, evidence preservation, status-update cadence, recovery checks, and a post-incident review. Rehearse the runbook. Notification duties and timelines must be confirmed against current law and contracts for the specific incident.
