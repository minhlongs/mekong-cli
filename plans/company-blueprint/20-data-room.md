# Step 20 — Data Room and Investor Materials

## Business
Maintain a private evidence room only when useful for a pilot, partner, grant, or investor diligence. Initial index:
1. Product and architecture overview with a reproducible demo.
2. License, contributor/IP records, and dependencies.
3. Security, privacy, AI governance, and incident policies.
4. User research and pilot outcomes with consent.
5. Actual financial statements, invoices, refunds, and cost data.
6. Entity, tax, contracts, and cap-table documents if applicable.
7. Roadmap and risk register.

No investor deck or financial projection is approved by this blueprint. The $1M ARR goal is a target, not traction or a forecast.

## Agentic
Agents may organize documents, draft summaries, and check links. A human owner approves access, redacts personal data, and verifies every factual claim.

## Governance
Separate actuals, targets, and scenarios. Use least-privilege access, an access log, expiry dates, and a process to revoke access. Never place secrets or raw customer data in a general-purpose data room.
