# Step 19 — Industry Patterns and IPO Archetypes

## Business
Benchmark the product against categories that solve adjacent jobs: open-source agent runtimes, developer workflow tools, local-first automation, and small-business operations software. Compare user, core job, deployment, license, support, and current offer. Do not assert market share, valuation, revenue, or “first/best” status without a current primary source.

The National Statistics Office of Vietnam reports 297.5 thousand newly established and resumed enterprises in 2025. This is broad economic context only; it is not the number of solo founders, CLI buyers, or addressable customers. Do not use it as TAM. Build market estimates bottom-up from a defined segment and observed willingness to pay.

## Agentic
Research agents can maintain a comparison matrix and flag changed pricing or terms. A human verifies every entry against official company product pages, filings, or public documentation before publication.

## Governance
Date every comparison and cite primary sources. For later IPO-readiness benchmarking, review exchange rules and issuer requirements only when a real entity and financing strategy exist; seek securities counsel.

**Official context source:** [Vietnam NSO, 2025 enterprise registration status](https://www.nso.gov.vn/en/data-and-statistics/2026/01/socio-economic-situation-in-the-fourth-quarter-and-2025/).
