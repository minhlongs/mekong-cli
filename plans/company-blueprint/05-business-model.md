# Step 05 — Business Model and Unit-Economics Hypotheses

## Business
**Archetype to test:** open-source developer runtime with a possible paid hosted, support, or usage-based offer. The monetization path is not yet validated. Keep the MIT-licensed runtime and any future commercial services clearly described and contractually distinct.

Do not publish the old $49/$149/$499 tiers or MCU allowances as current pricing; they are unverified planning history. Test one simple offer after interviews identify a costly, recurring job.

Track actuals before forecasting:
- Revenue: paid accounts, recurring revenue, refunds, and expansion.
- Cost to serve: model/API use, hosting, payment fees, support, and human review.
- Retention: repeated workflow use, renewal, downgrade, and cancellation.
- Acquisition: source, qualified conversations, trial/pilot conversion, and cash/time spent.

Use formulas, not assumed benchmarks: CAC = attributable sales and marketing cost / new paying accounts; gross margin = (revenue − direct delivery cost) / revenue; LTV requires observed retention and gross margin; payback requires cohort revenue and acquisition cost.

## Agentic
Revenue and finance agents can reconcile approved event data and draft cohort reports. The founder reviews definitions, exclusions, refunds, and source data before decisions.

## Governance
Report actuals separately from scenarios. Mark every price, margin, CAC, LTV, churn, market size, and conversion estimate as a hypothesis until supported by measured cohorts. The $1M ARR goal is aspirational and must not be presented as a projection.
