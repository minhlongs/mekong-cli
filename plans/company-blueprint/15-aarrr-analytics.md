# Step 15 — AARRR and Lean Analytics

## Business
At Zero→PSF, the North Star is **weekly verified workflow completions by target users**, paired with qualitative evidence that the outcome is useful. Once revenue exists, report retained paying accounts and recurring revenue alongside product value.

| Funnel area | Define and measure |
|---|---|
| Acquisition | Qualified source and first meaningful visit; do not equate traffic with demand |
| Activation | First successful completion of the selected workflow |
| Retention | Repeat completion by the same user over a defined period |
| Revenue | Cash-collected recurring revenue, net of refunds, with cohort and currency |
| Referral | Qualified users referred by an existing user, with consent |

## Agentic
OPS owns event definitions and dashboard quality. Engineering verifies collection and failure handling. Agents may summarize patterns but cannot silently alter metric definitions.

## Governance
Do not collect telemetry by default without a documented need and notice. Minimize and protect data. Keep actuals separate from targets; label CAC, LTV, churn, conversion, and gross margin as unavailable until measured. Define denominator, period, exclusions, and source for every metric.
