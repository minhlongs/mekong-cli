# Step 13 — Sales Process and Channels

## Business
Use a founder-led learning pipeline:
1. Identify a potential user who matches the ICP hypothesis.
2. Ask for a short problem interview; do not pitch before understanding current behavior.
3. Record the job, existing workaround, urgency, constraints, and decision process.
4. Invite a qualified user to a bounded design-partner pilot.
5. Agree on success measures, data boundaries, support, and any payment terms in writing.
6. Review use, repeat value, objections, and willingness to pay.
7. Close the loop with a next step or a clear no-fit reason.

Treat GitHub, technical communities, and referrals as hypotheses for acquisition, not proven channels.

## Agentic
AE/Research agents may prepare account research, interview summaries, and follow-up drafts. The founder approves outreach and sends it. Agents do not negotiate terms or represent commitments.

## Governance
Respect platform rules, consent, privacy, and opt-outs. Do not log personal data without a business need. Track stage definitions and actual outcomes; do not count downloads, stars, or demo requests as paid traction.
