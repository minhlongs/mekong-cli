# Step 08 — Content Pillars and Top-of-Funnel

## Business
Build a small, evidence-led content program for developers and solo founders:
1. Practical agent-runtime workflows and reproducible demos.
2. Reliability, verification, and governance for agentic work.
3. Local-first and provider-neutral architecture.
4. Founder experiments, including failed hypotheses and what changed.
5. Vietnam-focused workflows only when product behavior and regulatory claims are verified.

Begin with documentation, short demos, and technical articles tied to one target workflow. Avoid publishing unsourced market-size, savings, hiring-replacement, or revenue claims.

## Agentic
Agents can propose outlines, draft technical explanations, and repurpose approved material. Engineering verifies code and behavior; Governance checks citations, license, privacy, and claims; the founder approves publication.

## Governance
Use primary sources for laws, statistics, and product behavior. Link to versioned repository docs where possible. Obtain consent for user stories and remove sensitive data from examples.
