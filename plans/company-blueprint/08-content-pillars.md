## Channel Strategy

### 1. Blog: mekongmind.com/blog (NOT YET LAUNCHED)

- Long-form pillar posts (2,000-3,000 words) anchoring each pillar
- SEO-optimized for Vietnamese and English keywords
- Weekly cadence: one pillar post + one tactical post per week
- Bilingual titles, bilingual summaries, full English body with Vietnamese section headers
- Repurpose into Twitter threads, Indie Hackers posts, Reddit self-posts

### 2. Twitter/X: Build in Public

- Daily thread on building Mekong CLI and Sophia AI Factory
- Revenue numbers, user count, technical wins and failures
- Screenshots of actual dashboards and logs (authenticity > polish)
- Engage with #buildinpublic #indiehackers #solofounder communities
- Cross-link to blog posts for deeper dives
- Post in both English and Vietnamese

### 3. Indie Hackers: Case Studies

- Monthly case study format: problem → solution → results → lessons
- Focus on revenue numbers, growth tactics, and technical decisions
- Example titles:
  - "How I Built a Telegram Bot That Runs Marketing Campaigns"
  - "From $0 to $1K MRR with No Co-Founder"
  - "OpenRouter vs. Direct API: The Real Cost Comparison"
- Include Vietnamese takeaways for the local audience
- Respond to every comment within 24 hours

### 4. YouTube: Demo Videos

- Screen-recorded demos (no face, no studio setup)
- 5-10 minute format: problem intro → live demo → key takeaway
- Use ElevenLabs voiceover + D-ID avatar when narration is needed
- Title format: "How to [Action] with AI in [Time]"
- Vietnamese subtitles on all videos
- Publish one video per week to start, then assess retention

### 5. Hacker News: Launch Posts

- Launch announcements for major milestones (public beta, v1.0, revenue milestone)
- HN-optimized titles: factual, benefit-driven, no hype
- First comment should tell the backstory (solo founder, bootstrapped)
- Prepare for both positive and negative feedback
- Launch day checklist:
  - Blog post ready
  - HN title and first comment drafted
  - Monitoring for questions (reply within 30 minutes)
  - Link tracking set up for acquisition analysis
  - Vietnamese version ready for local press pickup

## TOF Strategy (Top of Funnel)

### The Hook Funnel

```
Social Post / Tweet → Blog Post → Email Capture → Pilot Offer
```

### TOF Channels by Priority

1. **Twitter/X** (highest ROI for solofounder) — daily threads, live demos, revenue screenshots
2. **Indie Hackers** (high-intent audience) — detailed case studies, revenue breakdowns
3. **Hacker News** (spiky launch traffic) — milestone launches only, 2-3 per year
4. **YouTube search** (evergreen) — problem-solving tutorials
5. **Blog SEO** (long-term compounding) — pillar content, Vietnamese + English keywords
