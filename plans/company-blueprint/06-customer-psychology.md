# Step 06 — Customer Psychology and Personas

## Business
**Initial ICP hypothesis:** technically capable solo founders or very small teams that already use a CLI and need a repeatable workflow across planning, execution, verification, and business operations.

Interview about recent behavior, not imagined interest:
- What task was last repeated, costly, delayed, or error-prone?
- What tools and human work are used now?
- What breaks trust in agent output?
- What would make the user try, pay for, and return to a solution?
- Which data, provider, deployment, or approval constraints matter?

Keep adjacent personas (Vietnamese small businesses, agencies, developers, and founders using MekongMind funnels) separate until interviews show shared jobs and buyers.

## Agentic
PM/Research agents can prepare neutral interview guides, synthesize consented notes, and cluster repeated pain. The founder conducts or reviews interviews and checks that summaries preserve dissent and uncertainty.

## Governance
Do not fabricate testimonials, customer quotes, buying triggers, or personas. Store only needed research data, record consent, redact personal details, and honor deletion requests. Label this ICP as a hypothesis until evidence supports it.
