# Customer Psychology + Personas

## ICP: The Tech-Savvy Solo Founder

- **Profile:** Developer or designer turned founder, 25-45, highly technical
- **Pain:** Juggling 10+ SaaS tools daily, no time for deep work, burn-out from operational overhead, wants AI to do the grunt work
- **Job To Be Done:** "Replace my entire team with AI agents so I can focus on product and customers"
- **Decision trigger:** "I spend more on SaaS subscriptions than I make" -- the month the math flips, they are ready to buy

## Persona 1: Indie Hacker

- **MRR:** $2K to $10K, solo operator, no employees
- **Tech stack:** VS Code + 15+ SaaS tools (Vercel, Supabase, Stripe, Mailchimp, Sentry, Linear, etc.)
- **Behavior:** Buys tools on credit card individually, churns fast if value not proven in first week
- **Objection:** "Can I trust an AI with my auth and billing?" -- needs security reassurance and demo
- **Wants:** All-in-one AI ops platform that replaces the tool stack with a single subscription
- **Sales channel:** Twitter/X, indie newsletters, Hacker News, Product Hunt
- **Buying signal:** Asks about API access, data portability, and cancellation terms before purchase

## Persona 2: AI Agency Owner

- **MRR:** $10K to $50K, 2 to 5 people, technical founder + junior ops
- **Tech stack:** Same as Indie Hacker plus client management tools (HubSpot, Asana, Notion)
- **Behavior:** Evaluates platforms for multi-client scalability, needs white-label capability
- **Objection:** "Will my clients know this is automated?" -- needs white-label, custom domain, branded output
- **Wants:** Scale without hiring more people, multi-tenant management, per-client billing
- **Sales channel:** LinkedIn, agency Slack communities, podcast sponsorships
- **Buying signal:** Asks about white-label, reseller pricing, and SLA guarantees

## Persona 3: Bootstrapped SaaS Founder

- **MRR:** $0 to $2K, pre-revenue or early stage, technical but stretched thin
- **Behavior:** Deep in build mode, has not hit product-market fit yet
- **Wants:** Fastest path to a working product, does not care about polish
- **Objection:** "I am not sure I will be around in 6 months" -- needs month-to-month, no lock-in
- **Trigger:** Shipping velocity drops because ops (billing, emails, support tickets) take too long

## Persona 4: Micro-SaaS Portfolio Owner

- **MRR:** $5K to $30K across 3 to 5 micro-SaaS products, solo or with one VA
- **Behavior:** Runs multiple small products, each with its own stack -- duplication is the pain
- **Wants:** Unified dashboard across products, shared AI agents, consolidated billing
- **Objection:** "Each product has different customers and domains" -- needs per-product isolation
- **Trigger:** End of month reconciliation takes 2 days across all products

## Persona 5: Ex-Startup CTO Turned Solopreneur

- **MRR:** $8K to $20K, consulting + product revenue, deeply technical
- **Behavior:** Has built and scaled teams before, now intentionally solo
- **Wants:** Enterprise-grade infrastructure without the team cost
- **Objection:** "I have been burned by over-promising AI tools before" -- needs verifiable results
- **Trigger:** Missed a customer SLA because operational tasks consumed the day

## Psychology Themes

### The SaaS Math Flip

All personas share a single arithmetic trigger: when monthly SaaS spend exceeds marginal revenue from the next hire, the purchase becomes rational. Mekong must surface this math in onboarding: "You spend $X/month on tools. Mekong replaces 12 of them. Payback period: Y weeks."

### Loss Aversion Over Gain

Solo founders fear losing existing customers more than they desire new efficiency. Position Mekong as risk reduction (downtime, missed invoices, churn from slow response) before efficiency gain.

### Identity Alignment

The ICP identifies as a builder, not an operator. Mekong's value prop must reinforce builder identity: "You build. We operate." Any messaging that suggests "you are now a manager" will trigger rejection.

### The Trust Barrier

Solo founders have been burned by AI hype. Every persona needs a trust bridge: live demo, transparent architecture overview, data residency commitments, and a generous free tier that proves value before payment.

## Funnel Mapping by Persona

1. **Awareness** -- Twitter/X post doing the SaaS math flip publicly
2. **Interest** -- Landing page with tool comparison table (Mekong vs 15 tools)
3. **Evaluation** -- Interactive ROI calculator with persona-specific defaults
4. **Trial** -- 14-day free tier with full features, no credit card
5. **Conversion** -- Email sequence triggered by trial activity milestones
6. **Retention** -- Monthly "SaaS savings report" showing reduced tool count and cost
