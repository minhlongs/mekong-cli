# Phase 04: Port Circular Waste Oil Recovery & Co-Processing

## Context & Standards
- MARPOL Annex I Reg 12/13, Basel Convention, Circular 02/2022/TT-BTNMT, Circular 39/2023/TT-BGTVT.

## Requirements
- Safety reception gate: Flash point $T_{\text{flash}} \ge 60.0^\circ\text{C}$ (Pensky-Martens closed cup). If $< 60^\circ\text{C}$, auto-reject.
- Thermal de-emulsification & centrifugal water separation: Separation efficiency >= 95.0%, effluent water <= 15.0 ppm.
- Recovered Fuel Oil (RFO) criteria: LHV >= 38.0 MJ/kg, sediment <= 0.5%, ash <= 0.15%.
- Circular economy CO2 offset: 1.2 kg CO2 eq saved per kg RFO recycled into industrial cement kilns.
