# Phase 03: Marine Biofouling Risk Assessment & In-Water Cleaning

## Context & Standards
- IMO Res. MEPC.378(80), BIMCO/ICS In-Water Cleaning Standard 2021, Vietnam QCVN 08-MT:2015/BTNMT.

## Requirements
- Level of Fouling scale (LoF 0 to 5): Floerl et al.
- Hydrodynamic power penalty: 2.5% to 35.0% added resistance.
- Regulatory permission matrix:
  - LoF <= 1: Grooming permitted.
  - LoF 2–3: In-water cleaning permitted ONLY with active capture/filtration >= 99.0% down to 5 um.
  - LoF >= 4: Prohibited in port waters; drydocking mandatory.
