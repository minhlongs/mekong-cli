# RESEARCH REPORT: PHASE 329 LIFTING & PHASE 330 OPRC

## Phase 329: Port Lifting Appliances & Cargo Handling Gear
**Statutory Framework**: ILO Convention 152 & QCVN 23:2010/BGTVT (Vietnam National Technical Regulation on Safety Inspection of Equipment).

### Proof Load Test Ratios
- **SWL <= 20 Tons**: 1.25 x SWL
- **20 Tons < SWL <= 50 Tons**: SWL + 5 Tons
- **SWL > 50 Tons**: 1.10 x SWL

### ISO 4309 Wire Rope Discard Criteria
- **Broken Wires**: Discard if visible broken wires exceed limit within a lay length (e.g., 6x rope diameter).
- **Diameter Reduction**: Discard if reduction >= 7% of nominal diameter.
- **Corrosion**: Visual pitting/surface oxidation grade; discard if structural integrity compromised by cross-section loss.
- **Survey Interval**: Initial/Annual load certification required; Quadrennial (5-year) thorough survey mandatory.

## Phase 330: Marine Oil Spill Response (OPRC 1990) & NEBA
**Tiered Capability Standards**:
- **Tier 1 (< 7 tons)**: Local terminal/port response capability.
- **Tier 2 (7 - 700 tons)**: Regional mutual aid agreements.
- **Tier 3 (> 700 tons)**: National/International emergency response.

### 화학 분산제 (Chemical Dispersant) NEBA Decision Matrix
- **Water Depth**: >= 10 meters.
- **Distance from Shore**: >= 3 Nautical Miles.
- **Oil Viscosity**: < 10,000 cSt (disperse effectiveness threshold).

### Containment Engineering
- **Boom Sizing**: Length L = 3 x Vessel LOA (standard deployment).
- **Tension Load**: $T = k \cdot v^n \cdot h$ (where $k$ is drag coeff, $v$ is current velocity, $h$ is boom depth; $n \approx 2$).
- **Recovery Rate**: $R_{net} = E \times R_{encounter}$ (where $E$ = skimmer efficiency, $R_{enc} = v \cdot w \cdot h$ [v=velocity, w=width, h=slick thickness]).
- **Skimmer Types**: Weir-type (volume suction) vs. Oleophilic (selective adhesion).
