# Research Report: Mekong Maritime Phases 373 & 374

## Phase 373: Marine Biofouling & In-Water Cleaning (IWC)
### Compliance & Regulatory Framework
- **Primary Standard**: IMO MEPC.378(80) (Biofouling Management).
- **Vietnam Integration**: Law on Environmental Protection 2020; QCVN 08-MT:2015/BTNMT (Water Quality Surface / Port Waters).
- **Operational Boundaries** (LoF Scale derived from Floerl et al.):
  - **LoF 0-1**: Acceptable for grooming.
  - **LoF 2-3**: IWC permitted ONLY with active capture/filtration system meeting 99% efficiency for particles >= 5um.
  - **LoF 4-5**: Drydock required; port-side IWC prohibited due to invasive species biosecurity risk.

### Technical Metrics
- **Added Resistance (ΔCF)**: Modeling penalty using powering penalty correlation (2.5% - 35% fuel consumption increase).

## Phase 374: Waste Oil Recovery & Co-Processing
### Compliance & Regulatory Framework
- **Primary Standard**: MARPOL Annex I Reg 12/13; Basel Convention.
- **Vietnam Integration**: Circular 02/2022/TT-BTNMT (Hazardous Waste); Circular 39/2023/TT-BGTVT (Reception Fees).
- **Safety Thresholds**:
  - **Flash Point**: Forced rejection if < 60.0°C (gasoline danger).

### Performance Metrics
- **Process Efficiency**: Water separation >= 95%; Oily-water phase discharge <= 15ppm.
- **Recovered Fuel Oil (RFO)**: LHV >= 38.0 MJ/kg; sediment <= 0.5%; ash <= 0.15%.
- **Environmental Impact**: CO2 offset = 1.2 kg CO2 eq saved per kg RFO recycled.

## Risk Assessment & Mitigation
- **Biofouling**: Improper filtration leads to severe environmental penalties (QCVN 08-MT). Risk: Invasive species transfer.
- **Waste Oil**: High water cut or low flash point contamination compromises kiln stability.

## Unresolved Questions
- Port-specific limits for RFO impurities under local Vietnam electricity regulations for cement kilns.
- Verification of 5um sensor availability for 2026 fleet deployment validation.
