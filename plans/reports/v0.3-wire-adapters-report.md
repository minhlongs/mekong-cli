## What Changed

### `__init__` signature
- `memory_store`, `telemetry` changed from required to `Optional` with `_default_*()` fallback
- `llm_router` added as new optional param with `_default_llm_router()` fallback
- `billing` remains required (duck-typed, CLI-injected)

### Three default factory methods added
- `_default_memory_store()` → `MemoryStoreAdapter()`
- `_default_telemetry()` → `TelemetrySinkAdapter()`
- `_default_llm_router()` → `LLMRouterAdapter()`

### hasattr removals (3 total)
| Location | Old guard | New call |
|---|---|---|
| `observe()` L195 | `hasattr(self._telemetry, "record_metric")` | `self._telemetry.emit({"event_type": "task_completed", ...})` |
| `remember()` L222 | `hasattr(self._memory_store, "append")` | `self._memory_store.store(key, json_bytes)` |
| `commit()` L234 | `hasattr(self._telemetry, "record_event")` | `self._telemetry.emit({"event_type": "run_completed", ...})` |

### hasattr retained (4 total — duck-typed, CLI-injected)

- `dispatcher.dispatch` (L184)
- `billing.record_usage` (L227)
- `billing.check_quota` (L230)
