# BWM & Decarbonization Statutory Report (Phases 327-328)

## 1. Ballast Water Management (Phase 327)

### IMO BWM D-2 Standards (Reg. D-2)
- **Organisms > 50 µm**: < 10 viable m⁻³
- **10–50 µm**: < 10 viable mL⁻¹
- **Indicator Microbes**:
  - Vibrio cholerae: < 1 cfu/100 mL
  - E. coli: < 250 cfu/100 mL
  - Intestinal Enterococci: < 100 cfu/100 mL

### USCG Type Approval (46 CFR 162.060)
- Uses FDA/CMF (Critical Membrane Filtration) staining for viability.
- FDA vs. Neutral Red: Strict "living/dead" discrimination.
- **UV Transmittance (UVT)**: Min 50-60% typically required for effective UV-C dosing.
- **Electrochlorination (TRO)**: Neutralization required (Sodium Thiosulfate) when TRO > 0.1 mg/L in discharge.

### Sediment & Stripping
- **Stripping**: BWM Reg. B-5 requires sediment disposal at reception facilities. Drydock stripping limit: Residue must be < 5% of tank volume to prevent re-ballasting contamination.

## 2. Decarbonization & Alternative Fuels (Phase 328)

### EU ETS Maritime (2023/959)
- **Applicability**: Vessels ≥ 5,000 GT.
- **Surrender Phase-in**: 40% (2024), 70% (2025), 100% (2026+).
- **Formula**: $EUA = \sum (Emissions \times Scope\_Factor)$. Scope 1 = 100% voyages (EU/EEA), 50% voyages (External).

### FuelEU Maritime (2023/1805)
- **GHG Intensity Limit**: 2025: -2%, 2030: -6%, 2050: -80% (vs 2020 baseline of 91.16 gCO2eq/MJ).
- **Pooling (Art 28)**: Companies can pool ships; average intensity must meet the limit.
- **Banking**: Surplus credits from year N can be banked to year N+1 (limit 3 years).

### Safety Zones (IGC/IGF/Methanol guidelines)
- **Leak Thresholds**: 
  - Methanol: < 200 ppm (TWA 8hr).
  - Ammonia: < 25 ppm (TWA 8hr), 50 ppm (STEL 15min).
- **Nitrogen Purge**: O2 must be < 2% (vol) before fuel introduction.
- **Safe Radii**: Calculated based on potential jet fire or toxic cloud dispersion (typically 5-15m for bunker stations depending on pressure/velocity).

**Unresolved Questions**:
- Regulatory alignment on "viable" definition between USCG (FDA) and IMO (New IMO G8 guidelines).
- Impact of specific synthetic e-fuel accounting in EU methodology.
