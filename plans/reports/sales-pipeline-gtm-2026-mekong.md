| Stage | Name | Duration | Conversion | Activities |
|-------|------|----------|------------|------------|
| **S1** | **Lead Generated** | Day 0 | 100% | Inbound signup, outbound prospecting, event lead |
| **S2** | **Qualified (MQL)** | Day 1-3 | 60% | Discovery call, pain point identified, budget confirmed |
| **S3** | **Demo Completed** | Day 4-7 | 40% | Live CLI demo, ROI calculator, use case mapping |

| **S6** | **Closed Won** | Day 30-45 | 10% | Contract signed, billing setup, onboarding scheduled |
