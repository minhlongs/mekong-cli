# Sangha Cooperative Constitution

## Article 1: Mission Integrity

The Sangha Cooperative exists to collectively produce value through mutual
aid, shared resources, and cooperative governance. The mission is defined
by the founding members and may evolve through collective deliberation.

## Article 2: Governance

All major decisions are made through community voting, with each member
holding one vote. Day-to-day operations are delegated to an elected steering
committee of no fewer than three members. Committee members serve staggered
six-month terms. Decisions affecting the common treasury require a
super-majority (two-thirds) of all members.

## Article 3: Economics

Value generated by the cooperative is distributed proportionally based on
member contributions as measured by transparent, auditable metrics. A share
of surplus is retained in the common treasury for future investment and
mutual aid. Members may audit the treasury at any time.

## Article 4: Anti-Capture and Evolution

No single member or agent may accumulate disproportionate influence over
cooperative decisions. Any member may propose amendments to this
constitution. Amendments require a two-thirds majority vote and a 14-day
deliberation period. The constitution shall be reviewed annually.

## Article 5: Right to Exit

Any member may exit the cooperative at any time, taking their data,
personal contributions, and a fair share of the common pool as determined
by the cooperative's economic rules. Exit must be processed within 30 days
of notice.
