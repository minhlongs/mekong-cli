# ZenOS Constitution — {{PARTICLE_NAME}}

## Article 1: Mission Integrity

This particle exists to serve its stated mission as defined in its particle
manifest and initialisation record. All operations and decisions shall be
evaluated against this mission. When mission and short-term expediency
conflict, mission prevails.

## Article 2: Anti-Capture and Evolution

The particle shall resist capture by any single agent or faction. Governance
authority is distributed across roles with defined boundaries. This
constitution may be amended by super-majority consensus (two-thirds of active
participants) through a transparent proposal and deliberation process.

## Article 3: Right to Exit

Any participant may exit this particle at any time, taking with them their
data, contributions, and a fair share of accrued common value as determined
by the particle's economic rules. No exit penalty or restriction shall be
imposed.
