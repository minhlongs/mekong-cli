# SOP: Shared Review Cycles
**Version:** 1.0.0 | **Applies to:** All Layers

## Intent
Standardize review cadence across all business operations.

## Review Types

### Daily Standup (CEO + active agents)
- What was completed yesterday
- What's planned today
- Blockers

### Weekly Business Review (all layers)
- Metrics vs targets
- Agent status
- Priority alignment

### Monthly Strategy Review (CEO)
- Revenue vs target
- Product roadmap progress
- Team/agent performance
- Market/competitive changes

### Quarterly Planning (CEO)
- Quarterly OKRs
- Budget review
- Hiring/agent scaling plan

## Review Outputs
- All reviews produce a written summary in `reviews/`
- Decisions and action items with owners and deadlines
- Previous review action items checked off before new review starts

## Anti-Patterns
- Review without data (always pull metrics first)
- Review without decisions (every review must produce ≥1 decision)
- Review without follow-up (action items tracked to completion)
