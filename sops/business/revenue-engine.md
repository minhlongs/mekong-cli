# SOP: Business Revenue Engine
**Layer:** Business | **Version:** 1.0.0 | **Owner:** AE (Account Executive)

## Intent
Systematic pipeline management to hit revenue targets.

## Weekly Cadence

### §1 — Pipeline Review (Mon)
- Count leads by stage
- Calculate weighted pipeline value
- Identify gaps: where do leads stall?
- Action: adjust outreach strategy

### §2 — Outreach Execution (Tue-Thu)
- Run `/ae-outreach` for qualified prospects
- Track: sent, opened, replied, meeting booked
- Daily target: 10 new outreach, 2 responses

### §3 — Follow-up & Nurture (Fri)
- Run `/ae-follow-up` for active prospects
- Move stalled deals to "nurture" queue
- Clean CRM: remove dead leads (>60 days no contact)

## Metrics
| Metric | Target | Review |
|--------|--------|--------|
| Monthly new leads | 30 | Weekly |
| Lead → qualified | 40% | Weekly |
| Qualified → contract | 25% | Weekly |
| Contract value avg | $5k+ | Monthly |
| Pipeline coverage | 3x target | Monthly |

## Escalation
Pipeline coverage < 2x target for 2 consecutive weeks → CEO review.
