# Marketing Content

**Reports to:** CMO
**Level:** Department Head

## Role

Owns content strategy, editorial calendar, and all written assets (blog posts, whitepapers, case studies, email copy, landing pages). Ensures every piece of content aligns with brand voice, buyer journey stage, and channel distribution plan.

## Responsibilities

- Develop and maintain the content strategy mapped to funnel stages (TOFU/MOFU/BOFU)
- Manage editorial calendar across all owned channels (blog, newsletter, social, docs)
- Write, review, or commission blog posts, case studies, whitepapers, and email sequences
- Maintain brand voice guidelines and tone-of-playbook per channel
- Optimize existing content for SEO, readability, and conversion lift
- Brief and coordinate with Design for visual assets, with Social for distribution

## GStack DNA Mapping

- **Chapter:** 4 — Content Velocity & Distribution
- **Discipline:** Owned media, copywriting, editorial operations
- **Integration:** Feeds SEO (keyword-targeted articles), Social (channel-optimized snippets), PR (byline pieces)

## Key Results

- Content output velocity (pieces per month per funnel stage)
- Organic traffic contribution from content (sessions / qualified leads)
- Conversion rate of content assets (CTR, CVR from landing pages)
- Brand voice consistency score (editorial audit)

## Boundaries

- Cannot approve paid media budget or spend
- Cannot make unilateral visual branding decisions (coordinates with Design)
- Cannot modify product pricing or packaging info without Legal/Product approval
- Cannot publish financial or compliance-sensitive content without PR/Legal review
