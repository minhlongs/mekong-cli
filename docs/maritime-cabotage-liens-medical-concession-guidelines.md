# Maritime Domestic Cabotage, Liens Waterfall, Seafarer Medical & Seaport PPP Concession Guidelines (Phases 251-254)

## Overview & Statutory Framework
This document defines the mathematical models, statutory constraints, and operational business logic for:
1. **Phase 251: Maritime Domestic Cabotage Waiver & Foreign Vessel Licensing** (VMC 2015 Arts. 7, 21; Decree 58/2017/NĐ-CP Arts. 18-24).
2. **Phase 252: Maritime Liens Priority Waterfall & Judicial Ship Auction Proceeds** (VMC 2015 Arts. 37-43; MLC 1993; Law on Civil Judgment Enforcement).
3. **Phase 253: Seafarer Medical Fitness Certification & STCW Reg. I/9 Compliance** (STCW 1978/2010 Reg. I/9, MLC 2006 Standard A1.2, Circular 22/2017/TT-BYT).
4. **Phase 254: Seaport PPP Concession Contracts & Revenue-Sharing Waterfall** (Law on PPP 2020 No. 64/2020/QH14, Decree 35/2021/NĐ-CP, Decree 28/2021/NĐ-CP).

---

## 1. Phase 251: Maritime Domestic Cabotage Waiver
- **Cabotage Rule**: Domestic sea transport between Vietnamese ports is strictly reserved for Vietnamese-flagged ships.
- **Waiver Justifications**:
  1. *Specialized Cargo*: Liquefied gas (LNG/LPG), specialized chemicals, offshore project super-heavy cargo (>500 tonnes).
  2. *Fleet Deficiency*: Domestic fleet is unavailable or incapable of meeting transport schedules within 7 business days.
  3. *International Cruise*: Foreign passenger ships transporting international tourists along Vietnamese coastal ports.
- **Permit Validity**: Single voyage or fixed-term (up to 12 months maximum).
- **Statutory Fee**: Based on vessel gross tonnage (GT) and permit duration under Circular 39/2023/TT-BGTVT.

## 2. Phase 252: Maritime Liens Priority Waterfall
- **Court Carve-Out (Tier 0)**: Judicial custody, admiralty marshal, survey, and auction expenses deducted first.
- **Statutory Waterfall (VMC 2015 Art. 37)**:
  1. *Tier 1*: Seafarer wages, repatriation costs, social security contributions.
  2. *Tier 2*: Claims for loss of life or personal injury in direct connection with the operation of the vessel.
  3. *Tier 3*: Marine salvage rewards and General Average contributory shares.
  4. *Tier 4*: Port dues, canal tolls, pilotage, and maritime safety charges.
  5. *Tier 5*: Physical property loss/damage caused by the vessel (collision, port installations).
  6. *Tier 6 (Registered Mortgages)*: Validly registered ship mortgages (VMC Art. 38).
  7. *Tier 7 (General Maritime Claims)*: Bunker suppliers, ship repairers, agents, unsecured creditors.
- **Limitation Period**: Extinguishes in 1 year unless the ship has been arrested or judicially auctioned.

## 3. Phase 253: Seafarer Medical Fitness (STCW Reg. I/9)
- **Visual Acuity**:
  - Deck Officers / Lookouts: Snellen >= 6/6 (better eye) & >= 6/9 (other eye); Ishihara 24-plate color vision test (zero red/green confusion).
  - Engine / General Crew: Snellen >= 6/12 each eye; color perception adequate for alarm recognition.
- **Auditory Threshold**: Whisper test at 5.0m; pure tone audiometry <= 30 dB hearing loss across 500-2000 Hz.
- **Physical & Cardiovascular Fitness**: Blood pressure <= 140/90 mmHg; resting heart rate 60-100 bpm; BMI <= 32.0; agility test (ladder climbing, watertight door passage).
- **Certification Terms**:
  - Full fitness: 24 months validity (12 months if age < 18).
  - Fit with restrictions: Corrective lenses required, coastal trade only, daytime watch only.
  - Temporarily unfit / Permanently unfit.

## 4. Phase 254: Seaport PPP Concession & Revenue-Sharing
- **Concession Models**: BOT, BTO, BOO, O&M.
- **Financial Viability**:
  - Equity ratio >= 15% of total capital expenditure (CAPEX).
  - Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR) >= 1.20x.
  - Project NPV >= 0, IRR >= weighted average cost of capital (WACC).
- **Decree 35/2021/NĐ-CP Revenue-Sharing Formula**:
  - If actual revenue $R_{\text{actual}} < 0.75 \cdot R_{\text{plan}}$: State shares $50\% \cdot (0.75 \cdot R_{\text{plan}} - R_{\text{actual}})$.
  - If actual revenue $R_{\text{actual}} > 1.25 \cdot R_{\text{plan}}$: Private concessionaire pays State $50\% \cdot (R_{\text{actual}} - 1.25 \cdot R_{\text{plan}})$.
  - Neutral band: $0.75 \cdot R_{\text{plan}} \le R_{\text{actual}} \le 1.25 \cdot R_{\text{plan}}$ (no sharing).
