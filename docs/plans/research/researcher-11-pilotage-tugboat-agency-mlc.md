# Research Report 11: Maritime Pilotage, Tugboat Tariffs & Ship Agency MLC 2006 Compliance

**Domain:** Maritime Transport, Pilotage Services, Tugboat Assistance & Ship Husbandry  
**Applicable Legal Frameworks:**
- Circular 39/2023/TT-BGTVT (Tariffs for pilotage, tugboat and port services in Vietnamese seaports).
- Circular 54/2018/TT-BGTVT (Base tariffs for seaport services).

- Decree 160/2016/NĐ-CP & Decree 86/2020/NĐ-CP (Conditions for maritime transport and ship agency businesses).
- Decree 121/2014/NĐ-CP & Labour Code 2019 (Seafarer working conditions and SEA).

## 1. Maritime Pilotage Tariff Model (Circular 39/2023/TT-BGTVT)

### Pilotage Tariff Formula
For international voyages:
$$\text{Pilotage Fee (USD)} = \max(\text{GT}, \text{GT}_{\min}) \times d \times R_{\text{pilot}} \times \prod K_i$$
Where:
- $\text{GT}_{\min} = 2,000 \text{ GT}$ for international sea-going vessels (Article 5).
- $d$: Distance in nautical miles (hải lý / NM) from pilot boarding station to terminal berth/anchorage.
- $R_{\text{pilot}}$: Base rate per GT-NM (Area I: $0.0034 \text{ USD/GT-NM}$, Area II: $0.0032 \text{ USD/GT-NM}$, Area III: $0.0031 \text{ USD/GT-NM}$, offshore oil terminals: $0.0038 \text{ USD/GT-NM}$).
- Specialized Surcharge Multipliers ($K_i$):
  - Night Pilotage (18:00 to 06:00): $+50\%$ ($K_{\text{night}} = 1.50$).
  - IMO Dangerous Goods / Hazardous Gas / Chemical Tanker: $+10\%$ ($K_{\text{imo}} = 1.10$).
  - Vessel without operational main engine (Dead Ship / Tàu mất chủ động): $+150\%$ ($K_{\text{dead}} = 2.50$).
  - Pilot waiting / detention time: $10 \text{ USD/hour}$ after 1 free hour.

## 2. Tugboat Assistance & Towage Tariff Engine

### Tugboat Capacity Brackets & Hourly Rates (TT 39/2023)
Vessels entering/leaving berths require mandatory tug assistance based on LOA (Length Overall) and DWT/GT:
- Tug Capacity Classes:
  - Class A: $< 1,000 \text{ HP}$ ($1,800,000 \text{ VND/h}$ or $80 \text{ USD/h}$).
  - Class B: $1,000 - 2,000 \text{ HP}$ ($3,200,000 \text{ VND/h}$ or $140 \text{ USD/h}$).
  - Class C: $2,001 - 3,000 \text{ HP}$ ($4,800,000 \text{ VND/h}$ or $210 \text{ USD/h}$).
  - Class D: $3,001 - 4,000 \text{ HP}$ ($6,500,000 \text{ VND/h}$ or $280 \text{ USD/h}$).
  - Class E: $> 4,000 \text{ HP}$ ($8,500,000 \text{ VND/h}$ or $370 \text{ USD/h}$).
- Minimum billing time: 2.0 hours per maneuver.
- Standby / Overtime surcharge: $+30\%$ for operations on public holidays or weekends.

## 3. Ship Agency & Seafarer MLC 2006 Working Hours Engine

### Ship Agency Husbandry & Fee Schedules (Decree 160/2016/NĐ-CP)
- Base Agency Fee based on Vessel Gross Tonnage:
  - $\le 5,000 \text{ GT}$: $600 \text{ USD/call}$.
  - $5,001 - 15,000 \text{ GT}$: $1,000 \text{ USD/call}$.
  - $15,001 - 35,000 \text{ GT}$: $1,500 \text{ USD/call}$.
  - $> 35,000 \text{ GT}$: $2,200 \text{ USD/call}$.
- Daily Husbandry Surcharge: $150 \text{ USD/day}$ beyond 3 days in port.
- Cash-to-Master (CTM) Handling Commission: $1.5\%$ of disbursed cash amount.

### Seafarer Rest Hours & Maximum Sea Service (MLC 2006 Reg. 2.1 & 2.3)
- **Maximum Continuous Sea Service Period**: 11 months (330 days). Repatriation mandatory if exceeded.
- **Minimum Rest Hours**:
  - Minimum 10 hours of rest in any 24-hour period.
  - Minimum 77 hours of rest in any 7-day period.
  - Daily rest may be divided into no more than two periods, one of which must be at least 6 hours in length, and the interval between consecutive periods of rest shall not exceed 14 hours.
