# Research Report: Ship Classification, Surveys & Registration (Phases 195-196)

## 1. Statutory Grounding
- **Vietnam Maritime Code 2015 (Law No. 95/2015/QH13)**: Chapter II (Sea-going Vessels), Articles 14–38.
- **Decree 171/2016/NĐ-CP & Decree 86/2020/NĐ-CP**: Regulations on registration of sea-going vessels, bareboat charter flags, and statutory mortgage recording.
- **Circular 40/2016/TT-BGTVT & Circular 17/2023/TT-BGTVT**: Vietnam Register (VR) procedures for statutory technical survey of sea-going ships under Harmonized System of Survey and Certification (HSSC).

## 2. Core Mathematical & State Logic
- **HSSC Survey Windows**:
  - Annual Survey: Certificate anniversary date $\pm 3$ months.
  - Intermediate Survey: Between 2nd and 3rd anniversary dates (or within $\pm 3$ months of 2nd/3rd anniversary).
  - Renewal/Class Survey: Every 5 years (window: 3 months before expiry).
  - Bottom / Drydock Survey: Exactly 2 surveys in a 5-year cycle, maximum interval between surveys $\le 36$ months.
- **Condition of Class (CoC)**: Outstanding recommendations with defined due dates. If due date passes without rectification, certificate status automatically drops to `SUSPENDED` or `EXPIRED`.
- **Registration & Encumbrance Priority**:
  - Formal registration checklist: Bill of sale/builder certificate, cancellation certificate (if imported), classification certificate, tonnage certificate.
  - Mortgage recording priority: First-in-time timestamp priority under Decree 171 Art. 21. Bareboat charter registration permits temporary Vietnamese flag flying with lessor consent.
