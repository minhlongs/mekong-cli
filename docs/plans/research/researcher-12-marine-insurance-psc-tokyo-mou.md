## 1. Marine Insurance (H&M / P&I) & Constructive Total Loss (CTL)

### Hull & Machinery (H&M) Claim Settlement
- **Particular Average (PA) Net Claim Formula**:
  $$\text{Claim}_{\text{net}} = \max(0, \text{Agreed Repair Cost} - \text{Policy Deductible})$$
- **Constructive Total Loss (CTL) Assessment**:
  - A vessel is deemed a Constructive Total Loss when the reasonable cost of recovery and/or repair exceeds $80\%$ (or $100\%$ under standard English law) of the Insured Agreed Value.
  - Notice of Abandonment (Thông báo từ bỏ tàu) must be tendered unconditionally in writing under Article 326 of the Vietnam Maritime Code 2015 within 180 days of casualty.
- **P&I Pollution & Bunker Liability**:
  - Bunker Oil Pollution limit based on vessel GT:
    - For GT $\le 2,000$: $1.51 \text{ million SDR}$.
    - For GT $> 2,000$: $1.51 \text{ million SDR} + 604 \text{ SDR/GT}$ (up to $30,000 \text{ GT}$) $+ 453 \text{ SDR/GT}$ (above $30,000 \text{ GT}$).

## 2. Tokyo MOU Port State Control (PSC) Inspection Risk Profiling

### New Inspection Regime (NIR) Ship Risk Profile (SRP)
Vessels calling Asian-Pacific ports are classified into:
1. **High Risk Ships (HRS)**: Inspection window every 2 to 4 months.
2. **Standard Risk Ships (SRS)**: Inspection window every 5 to 8 months.
3. **Low Risk Ships (LRS)**: Inspection window every 9 to 18 months.

### Generic Weighting Factor Calculation (Target Factor / Risk Points)
- **Ship Type Multiplier**: Oil/Chemical Tanker, Gas Carrier, Bulk Carrier (>12 years) = $+2$ points. Passenger vessel = $+2$ points. General cargo = $+1$ point.
- **Ship Age Multiplier**:
  - $> 12$ years: $+1$ point.
  - $> 20$ years: $+2$ points.
  - $> 25$ years: $+3$ points.
- **Flag State Performance**:
  - Black List (High / Very High Risk): $+3$ points.
  - Grey List: $+1$ point.
  - White List: $0$ points.
- **Recognized Organization (RO / Classification Society)**:
  - Low performing RO: $+2$ points.
  - Medium/High performing RO: $0$ points.
- **Historic Deficiencies & Detentions**:
  - Any Detention in previous 36 months: $+3$ points per detention.
  - Outstanding Code 30 action (Ground for Detention) automatically elevates to HRS.
