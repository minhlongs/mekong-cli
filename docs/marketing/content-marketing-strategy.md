## Executive Summary

This content marketing strategy positions **Mekong IDE** as the enabling platform for the "one-person billion-dollar company" vision. We target solo founders and solopreneurs who want to replace a 50-person team with autonomous AI agents.

**Core Strategy:** Educational content → Product awareness → Community building → Conversion

**Key Differentiators:**
- 10 business layers in one subscription
- Runs locally (zero cloud cost) or cloud
- 290+ commands across all business functions
- Open source with paid tiers for credits/support

## 2. Target Audience Segments (Founder Personas)

### Persona 1: Technical Solo Founder (Primary)
**Demographics:** 25-35, CS degree or self-taught, comfortable with CLI/APIs  
**Goals:** Build MVP quickly, automate operations, reduce burn rate  
**Pain Points:** Can't afford team, overwhelmed with operational tasks  
**Content Preference:** Technical docs, GitHub repos, Hacker News, Reddit  
**Conversion Path:** Developer docs → Free trial → Starter tier → Upgrade

---

### Persona 2: Indie Maker (Secondary)
**Demographics:** 20-40, various backgrounds, often part-time  
**Goals:** Build side project → full-time income, maintain freedom  
**Pain Points:** Limited time, scattered tools, manual processes  
**Content Preference:** Twitter/X, Indie Hackers, YouTube tutorials  
**Conversion Path:** Success stories → No-code demos → Growth tier

---

### Persona 3: Small Agency Owner (Tertiary)
**Demographics:** 30-50, running 2-5 person agency  
**Goals:** Scale without hiring, deliver more client work  
**Pain Points:** Client management overhead, project profitability  
**Content Preference:** LinkedIn, industry newsletters, webinars  
**Conversion Path:** ROI calculator demo → Pro tier with custom agents

---

### Persona 4: Pre-founder (Awareness Stage)
**Demographics:** 25-45, considering founding, still employed  
**Goals:** Validate idea, understand founder journey  
**Pain Points:** Analysis paralysis, fear of failure, unclear path  
**Content Preference:** Podcasts, newsletters, Twitter threads  
**Conversion Path:** Educational content → Newsletter → Waitlist → Launch

## 3. Content Types & Formats

### Blog Content
- **Frequency:** 2-3 posts/week
- **Length:** 1,500-4,000 words (mix of quick tips and deep dives)
- **Formats:**
  - How-to guides (50%)
  - Thought leadership (25%)
  - Product announcements (15%)
  - Community highlights (10%)
- **SEO Focus:** Long-tail keywords around "solo founder," "AI agents," "business automation"

### Video Content
- **Platform:** YouTube (primary), LinkedIn, TikTok (short-form)
- **Frequency:** 1 long-form/week, 2-3 short-form/week
- **Types:**
  - Tutorials (15-20 min)
  - Use case walkthroughs (10-15 min)
  - Product demos (5-10 min)
  - Short tips (60-90 sec)
- **Production:** Screen recordings with voiceover initially; professional production for key content

### Newsletter
- **Platform:** ConvertKit/Substack
- **Frequency:** Weekly
- **Content:**
  - Product updates
  - Community wins
  - Curated founder resources
  - Upcoming features
- **Subscribers:** Target 1,000 by month 3, 5,000 by month 6

### Social Media
- **LinkedIn:** 3-5 posts/week (B2B focus, founder stories, thought leadership)
- **Twitter/X:** Daily (product tips, community engagement, thread series)
- **Indie Hackers:** 3 posts/week (success stories, transparent metrics)
- **Hacker News:** Monthly deep-dive posts (technical content)
- **Reddit:** Weekly engagement in relevant subreddits (r/startups, r/SideProject)

### Documentation
- **Comprehensive guides** for each of the 10 business layers
- **API reference** with interactive examples
- **Plugin development** tutorials
- **Migration guides** from other tools

### Lead Magnets
- **"The Solo Founder's Playbook"** (50-page PDF)
- **"Command Reference Cheat Sheet"** (interactive web app)
- **"PEV Engine Explained"** video course
- **"Constitutional AI Principles"** whitepaper

### SEO Tactics
- **Technical SEO:** Schema markup, fast page speed (<2s LCP), mobile-first
- **On-page:** Keyword in title, H1, meta description, URL, alt text
- **Content clusters:** Pillar pages for each business layer, supporting articles
- **Internal linking:** Dense linking between related content
- **External linking:** Authoritative outbound links, pursue backlinks
- **Local SEO:** Google Business Profile for any physical presence

## 11. Content Repurposing Strategy

**Maximize each piece of content:**

```
Blog post (3,000 words)
├── Twitter thread (10-15 tweets)
├── LinkedIn article (edited version)
├── YouTube video (script → narration + slides)
├── Newsletter feature
├── Instagram carousel (key points)
├── TikTok snippets (60-second tips)
├── Twitter Spaces topic
└── Podcast episode
```

**Production workflow:**
1. Create core content (blog or video)
2. Extract quotes, stats, visuals
3. Format for each platform
4. Schedule over 2-4 weeks
5. Track performance by format

## 12. Success Indicators & Optimization

### When to Pivot
- **Low engagement** (<1% on social, <30s avg. watch time): Change content format/topic
- **Low conversion** (<1% trial from visitors): Improve CTAs, landing pages, or targeting
- **High churn** (>10% newsletter unsubscribes): Re-evaluate content relevance
- **Low SEO traction** (<50 organic visitors/month after 6 months): Adjust keyword strategy

### Continuous Optimization
- **Weekly:** Review analytics, identify top content
- **Monthly:** A/B test headlines, CTAs, formats
- **Quarterly:** Strategy review, adjust pillars/themes
- **Semi-annually:** Major refresh, new pillar assessment

### C. Content Style Guide

**Voice & Tone:**
- Confident but not arrogant
- Technical but accessible
- Visionary but grounded
- Educational, not salesy

**Writing Guidelines:**
- Use "you" and "your" - conversational
- Explain jargon when first used
- Include concrete examples
- Use analogies for complex concepts
- Keep paragraphs under 3 sentences
- Use numbered lists and bullet points

**Visual Guidelines:**
- Brand colors: [from design system]
- Font: Inter for body, headline font for headers
- Images: Authentic, not stock-photo generic
- Screenshots: Annotated with arrows/callouts
