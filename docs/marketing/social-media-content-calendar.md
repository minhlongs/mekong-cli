# Social Media Content Calendar

**Purpose:** Maintain consistent posting across platforms while maximizing content repurposing

**Platforms & Posting Frequency:**
- **Twitter/X:** Daily (1-2 posts)
- **LinkedIn:** 3x per week
- **YouTube:** 1 long-form/week, 2 shorts/week
- **Newsletter:** Weekly (Friday)
- **Blog:** 2-3x per week

---

## Engagement Playbook

### Daily (15 min)
- Respond to all mentions/replies
- Engage with 10 relevant posts
- Share community content

### Weekly (1 hour)
- Thank new followers
- Feature user content
- Host Twitter Spaces (Thursdays)
- Review analytics

### Monthly (2 hours)
- Community spotlight newsletter feature
- Q&A session (YouTube Live or Twitter Spaces)
- Content planning based on top performers

## Crisis Response Guidelines

- **Negative comments:** Respond professionally within 2 hours, move to DM if needed
- **Feature complaints:** Acknowledge, explain roadmap, offer workaround
- **Competitor comparisons:** Focus on our value, never disparage
- **Misinformation:** Correct politely with sources
- **Trolls:** Do not engage (block/mute)
