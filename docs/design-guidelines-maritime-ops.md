# Design Guidelines — Maritime Pilotage, Husbandry, P&I & Tokyo MOU PSC Suite

**Visual Theme & Tokens:**
- **Primary Color:** Maritime Navy (`#0A2540`)
- **Accent High Risk / Detention:** Alert Amber / Crimson (`#DC2626` / `#F59E0B`)
- **Accent Safety / Compliant:** Oceanic Emerald (`#059669`)
- **Background:** Deep Slate Glass (`#0F172A` / `#1E293B`)
- **Typography:** Inter / JetBrains Mono for calculation numbers and nautical units.

**Key Dashboard Panels:**
1. **Pilotage & Tugboat Dispatch Calculator**: Dynamic GT-NM tariff lookup, night shifts, dead ship multiplier, and tugboat horsepower class allocation.
2. **Ship Agency & Seafarer MLC 2006 Monitor**: Active crew rest hours logging (24h/7d compliance), CTM disbursement tracking, and seafarer repatriation alert.
3. **Marine Insurance & Claims Settlement Studio**: H&M Particular Average deductible assessment, Constructive Total Loss 80% threshold indicator, and P&I Bunkers Convention oil spill liability limits.
4. **Tokyo MOU PSC Risk Profiler & Target Factor Matrix**: Ship risk profile scoring (LRS, SRS, HRS), Flag/RO performance indices, and deficiency Code 30 detention risk evaluator.
