# Mekong IDE — Go-to-Market Strategy

**Task #87** — Comprehensive GTM strategy for one-person company platform

## Executive Summary

Mekong IDE is a **one-person company platform** that replaces a 50-person team with 22 autonomous departments for **$49-499/mo**. Target: solo founders, solopreneurs, indie makers.

**Core value prop:** One subscription = entire workforce. Run locally (Ollama) or cloud (API). Zero cloud cost when self-hosted.

**GTM approach:** Product-led growth (PLG) with community-first launch, direct sales for Growth/Pro, agency partnerships for scale.

**Target:** 1,000 paying customers in 12 months → $1.2M ARR at $120/mo average.

## 3. Positioning & Messaging

### Positioning Statement

**For** solo founders who need a full team but only have a budget for one tool,  
**Mekong IDE** is** a one-person company platform that replaces a 50-person team with 22 autonomous departments,  
**unlike** individual AI coding assistants or disconnected SaaS tools,  
**we** provide end-to-end autonomous execution from strategy to deployment, running locally or in the cloud.

### Messaging Hierarchy

**Level 1 (Elevator pitch):**
> "Replace your entire team with autonomous agents. One subscription. 22 departments. $49/mo."

**Level 2 (Value prop):**
> "Mekong IDE gives solo founders a 50-person team for the price of one tool. Finance, marketing, engineering, legal, HR — all autonomous. Run locally on your Mac with Ollama (zero cloud cost) or use our cloud API."

**Level 3 (Feature highlights):**
- 290+ commands across 22 departments
- Plan-Execute-Verify loop with quality gates
- Local LLM support (Ollama, MLX) or cloud (OpenRouter, Anthropic)
- 10-layer business architecture (Founder → Business → Product → Engineering → Ops → Studio → CTO → PM → Dev → Worker)
- Constitutional AI governance (ZenOS 9 principles)

**Level 4 (Technical differentiation):**
- Harness engineering: context budget rules, SOP hard gates, evals/observability
- Plugin architecture: extend with custom commands
- MCU billing: pay per mission, not per seat
- Multi-provider LLM router: OpenRouter → DashScope → DeepSeek → Anthropic → OpenAI → Ollama

## 7. Sales Strategy

### Self-Serve (Free → Starter → Growth)

- **Free tier** → 50 credits → no credit card
- **Starter ($49)** → 300 credits → instant Polar checkout
- **Growth ($149)** → 1,200 credits → instant checkout OR sales call

- Free user runs out of credits → upgrade prompt
- Starter user consistently hits 80% credit usage → upgrade nudge

### Sales-Assisted (Growth → Pro → Enterprise)

**Target:** 20% of Growth customers → Pro via demo call

**Process:**
1. Lead fills Calendly link (embedded in dashboard)
2. 30-minute discovery call (understand use case)
3. Custom demo (show relevant departments)
4. 14-day trial (Pro tier, 2,500 credits)
5. Close with annual prepayment discount (20% off)

**Sales team:** Founder initially, then hire first Sales hire at $10k MRR

**Quota:** $5k MRR per month (4-5 Pro deals)

### Enterprise (Custom)

- Annual contract ($20k-100k+)
- On-prem deployment, custom SLAs, dedicated CSM
- Negotiated via executive sponsor (CTO/Founder)

## 10. Metrics & KPIs

### North Star Metric

**Weekly Active Users (WAU)** running ≥1 paid command

**Why:** Measures actual value delivery, not just signups

**Target:** 500 WAU by month 12

### Funnel Metrics

| Stage | Metric | Target (M12) | Measurement |
|-------|--------|--------------|-------------|
| Awareness | Website visits/mo | 50,000 | Google Analytics |
| Interest | Free tier signups/mo | 1,000 | `tenant.count{plan=free}` |
| Activation | Run ≥3 commands in week 1 | 40% | custom event |
| Conversion | Free → Paid (30d) | 5% | `tenant.upgrade_rate` |
| Revenue | MRR | $120,000 | Stripe/Polar |
| Expansion | Upgrade rate (annual) | 25% | `tenant.tier_change` |
| Retention | Churn (annual) | 15% | `tenant.churn_rate` |
| Referral | K-factor | 0.3 | `tenant.referrals` |

### Unit Economics

**Assumptions:**
- CAC: $50 (blended paid + organic)
- Gross margin: 85% (cloud costs ~$0.01/credit for hosted, near-zero for local)
- Avg customer lifetime: 36 months
- ARPU target: $120/mo

**LTV:** $120 × 36 × 0.85 = $3,672  
**LTV:CAC:** 73:1 (excellent)

**Expansion revenue target:** 15% annual upgrade rate → +$50-200/mo per upgrade

## 11. Risks & Mitigations

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Price increase backlash (existing users) | Medium | High | Grandfather 6 months, communicate value clearly |
| Competitors undercut on price | Low | Medium | Emphasize 22-department value, not price |
| Scale tier cannibalizes Pro | Low | Medium | Position Pro as "unlimited" with highest SLA |
| VN pricing confusion (USD vs VND) | Medium | Medium | Clear label: "Giá USD, hiển thị VND" |
| Credit consumption faster than expected | High | High | Monitor usage, adjust allocations; overage pricing |
| Polar webhook issues with new tiers | Medium | High | Test thoroughly in staging pre-launch |
| Low free → paid conversion (<3%) | Medium | High | A/B test upgrade prompts, add incentives |
| LLM cost spike (cloud users) | Medium | Medium | Optimize router, encourage local LLM |
| Platform instability (early bugs) | High | Medium | Extensive testing pre-launch; rapid fix response |
| Founder bandwidth (single-threaded) | High | High | Delegate to agents early; automate ops |
