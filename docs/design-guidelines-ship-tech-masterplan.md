# Design Guidelines: Ship Technical & Seaport Masterplan Intelligence Suite

## 1. Visual Theme & Aesthetic Principles
- **Theme**: Oceanic Obsidian & Nautical Engineering. Deep maritime blues (`#0a192f`, `#0f2b48`), titanium whites (`#f8fafc`), and radar neon accents: Emerald (`#10b981`) for Cleared/In-Class, Amber (`#f59e0b`) for Survey Window / CoC Pending, Ruby (`#ef4444`) for Class Suspended / Grounding Risk.
- **Typography**: Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans for UI headers and numbers; JetBrains Mono for statutory certificate numbers, IMO identifiers, and mathematical formulations.
- **Layout**: 4-quadrant operational cockpit:
  1. HSSC Survey & Class Status Radar.
  2. Ship Registry & Mortgage Prioritization Ledger.
  3. Channel UKC & Squat Hydrodynamic Clearance Gauge.
  4. Decision 1579 Port Capacity & Berth Occupancy Heatmap.

## 2. Interactive Data Displays
- Real-time gauge for UKC margin vs statutory safety limit (0.3m).
- Visual timeline for 5-year HSSC survey cycles showing Annual (Y1, Y2, Y3, Y4), Intermediate (Y2-Y3), and Renewal (Y5).
- Priority stack for recorded maritime mortgages showing chronological priority.
