# Campaign Brief: "EcoSmart Home Energy Management"

---

## 1. Campaign Objective

### Primary Objective
**Drive 50,000 qualified leads for EcoSmart's AI-powered home energy management system within Q2 2025 (April–June), achieving a minimum 12% lead-to-demo conversion rate and a target CAC of ≤$85.**

### Secondary Objectives
| Objective | KPI | Target |
|-----------|-----|--------|
| Brand awareness | Unaided brand recall (post-campaign survey) | ≥22% among target segment |
| Content engagement | Avg. time on product page | ≥3:30 minutes |
| Email nurture | Open rate / Click-through rate | ≥38% / ≥4.5% |
| Social proof | User-generated content submissions | ≥500 authentic testimonials |
| Retargeting efficiency | Retargeting → demo booking rate | ≥8% |

### Business Context
- **Product**: EcoSmart Home Hub + mobile app — real-time energy monitoring, AI optimization, utility bill reduction (avg. 23%), carbon footprint tracking.
- **Price point**: $299 hardware + $9.99/mo subscription (first 3 months free).
- **Market entry**: Expansion from West Coast to National (US) rollout.
- **Competitive differentiator**: Only system with **predictive load-shifting** that integrates with utility demand-response programs for automatic bill credits.

---

## 2. Target Audience

### Primary Persona: "The Pragmatic Green Homeowner"
| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Demographics** | Age 32–52, household income $110K–$250K, homeowners (single-family), 60% married/partnered, 65% have children at home |
| **Geography** | Initial focus: CA, TX, FL, NY, CO, WA, MA (high electricity rates + strong solar adoption + utility incentive programs) |
| **Psychographics** | Value efficiency, tech-savvy but not early adopters, motivated by **tangible ROI** > ideology, research-heavy buyers, trust peer reviews & third-party certifications |
| **Pain Points** | • Unpredictable utility bills (esp. summer/winter peaks)
• "Black box" energy usage — no visibility into what drives cost
• Solar owners: not maximizing self-consumption / export value
• Time-poor — want "set & forget" automation, not another dashboard to watch |
| **Purchase Triggers** | • Rate hike notice from utility
• Post-solar install realization of suboptimal usage
• Home renovation / EV charger addition
• Peer recommendation / neighbor install |
| **Media Consumption** | • Podcasts: tech, home improvement, personal finance
• YouTube: DIY home tech, energy efficiency deep-dives
• Newsletters: Wirecutter, The Prepared, local utility comms
• Social: Instagram (home aesthetic), LinkedIn (professional credibility), Reddit (r/homeautomation, r/solar) |
| **Decision Journey** | 1. Problem awareness (bill shock) → 2. Passive research (3–8 weeks) → 3. Active comparison (2–3 brands) → 4. Demo/trial request → 5. Spouse/partner alignment → 6. Purchase |

### Secondary Personas
| Persona | Description | Angle |
|---------|-------------|-------|
| **Solar Maximizer** | Existing rooftop solar, wants to optimize self-consumption & battery arbitrage | "Turn your solar into a revenue asset" |
| **EV Early Adopter** | New EV owner facing charging cost surprise | "Charge smarter, pay less — automatically" |
| **Property Manager / Landlord** | Multi-unit portfolio, seeks submetering & tenant billing automation | "Cut admin time 80%, recover costs fairly" |

### Exclusion Criteria
- Renters without landlord approval pathway
- Households <$75K income (price sensitivity > value perception)
- Homes with <12 months ownership (low investment intent)
- Existing whole-home energy monitor users (Sense, Emporia) unless dissatisfied

## 3. Creative Brief

### Campaign Theme
**"Your Home's Hidden Paycheck"**

*Core insight*: Most homeowners overpay by $400–$1,200/year because their home *doesn't think* about energy timing. EcoSmart makes the home think for them — turning waste into savings, automatically.

### Key Message Pillars
| Pillar | Headline | Proof Point |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| **Invisible Savings** | "Save while you sleep. The only energy manager that works *for* you." | Avg. 23% bill reduction without behavior change |
| **Predictive Intelligence** | "Your home knows when electricity is cheap. Now it acts on it." | Patent-pending load-shifting + utility DR integration |
| **Set & Forget** | "Install once. Benefit forever. No dashboards to watch." | 94% of users never open app after week 2 — by design |
| **Future-Proof Investment** | "Works with solar, EV, battery, utility rates — today and tomorrow." | Matter/Thread certified, OTA updates, 5-yr warranty |

### Tone & Voice
- **Confident but not braggy** — we prove, we don't just claim
- **Clear over clever** — energy is complex; our job is to simplify
- **Empathetic to skepticism** — address "smart home fatigue" head-on
- **Visually grounded** — real homes, real meters, real bills (blurred)

### Creative Assets Required
| Format | Qty | Specs | Deployment |
|--------|-----|-------|------------|
| **Hero Video (60s)** | 1 | 4K, 16:9 + 9:16 cutdowns, CC in EN/ES | YouTube pre-roll, Meta/IG Reels, CTV, website hero |
| **Demo Explainer (90s)** | 1 | Screen-recorded app walkthrough + install timelapse | Product page, email nurture, sales enablement |
| **Testimonial Series** | 6 | 15s vertical, user-generated style (scripted but authentic) | Meta/IG Stories, TikTok Spark Ads, retargeting |
| **Static Carousel** | 8 | 1080x1080, 5-card carousel: Problem → Solution → Proof → Install → Savings | Meta/IG Feed, LinkedIn, Pinterest |
| **Comparison Infographic** | 3 | EcoSmart vs. Sense vs. Utility App vs. Manual tracking | Blog, SEO landing pages, PDF download |
| **Interactive Calculator** | 1 | Embeddable: zip code + bill amount → projected annual savings | Homepage, paid landing pages, partner sites |
| **Audio Ad (30s)** | 2 | Host-read style for podcast dynamic insertion | Energy, tech, finance podcasts (Midroll/Spotify) |
| **Direct Mail Kit** | 1 | Dimensional mailer: thermal-imaging postcard + QR to calculator | High-value target list (top 5% by propensity model) |

### Visual Language
- **Color palette**: Deep teal (#006D77), warm amber (#F77F00), clean white, charcoal (#2D2D2D) — *avoid generic "green tech" lime*
- **Photography**: Authentic homes (not staged), diverse families, visible hardware in context, utility meter close-ups, app screens in-situ
- **Iconography**: Minimal line-weight, motion-enabled for digital (Lottie)
- **Typography**: Inter (UI), Fraunces (display headlines) — geometric + humanist balance

| **Quarterly** | Attribution model update, persona validation, strategic pivot | Leadership |

### Creative Testing Roadmap (First 6 Weeks)
| Week | Test | Hypothesis |
|------|------|------------|
| 1–2 | Hook variations: "Bill shock" vs. "Solar waste" vs. "EV surprise" | Problem-specific hooks outperform generic "save energy" |
| 2–3 | Format: UGC testimonial vs. expert explainer vs. animation | UGC drives trust; expert drives consideration |
| 3–4 | Offer framing: "3 months free" vs. "$150 bill credit" vs. "Price lock guarantee" | Dollar-value framing beats time-based trial |
| 4–5 | Landing page: Calculator-first vs. Video-first vs. Comparison-first | Calculator-first increases qualified lead rate |
| 5–6 | Audience: Broad interest vs. Propensity modeled vs. Retargeting only | Modeled audience improves CPL by ≥20% |

### Compliance & Guardrails
- **Claims substantiation**: All savings claims backed by DOE/NREL methodology study (link in footer)
- **Utility partnership language**: "Works with [Utility] demand-response programs" — only where signed agreement exists
- **Data privacy**: CCPA/CPRA compliant; no PII in ad pixels; consent-first email capture
- **Accessibility**: WCAG 2.1 AA for all landing pages, captions on all video, alt text on all images
