### Governance: Binh Pháp (Sun Tzu)

13 chapters govern agent behavior:
- 始計 (Laying Plans) — strategic planning, resource allocation
- 作戰 (Waging War) — execution speed, cost optimization
- 謀攻 (Attack by Stratagem) — competitive intelligence
- 軍形 (Disposition) — market positioning
- 兵勢 (Momentum) — growth acceleration
- 虛實 (Void & Substance) — focus resources where competitors aren't
- 軍爭 (Military Contention) — sprint execution, shipping velocity
- 九變 (Nine Variations) — adapting to changing conditions
- 行軍 (Army on March) — process discipline
- 地形 (Terrain) — market segment analysis
- 九地 (Nine Terrains) — stage-appropriate strategy
- 火攻 (Fire Attack) — aggressive growth tactics
- 用間 (Intelligence) — data-driven decision making

### Escalation Levels
- L0: Agent decides autonomously (routine tasks)
- L1: Agent decides, notifies human (important but reversible)
- L2: Agent recommends, human approves (significant decisions)
- L3: Human decides (irreversible, high-stakes)
