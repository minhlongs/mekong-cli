# Outcome-Based Pricing Rationale — BizOps Department

## Market Reference Points

| Vendor | What They Charge | Annual Cost |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|
| BizOps Manager | $100,000-140,000/yr | $120,000/yr avg |
| RevOps platform (Clari) | $12,000-30,000/yr | $20,000/yr avg |
| Tableau / Looker | $1,700-2,400/user/yr | $17,000-24,000/yr (10 users) |
| Notion Business | $16/user/mo × 20 | $3,840/yr |

## Our Pricing Model

```
Base: $49/mo (200 credits)
Quarterly business review: $40
Revenue ops dashboard: $30
Hiring sprint coordination: $25
Weekly business report: $12
```

**Savings vs. BizOps Manager:** $120,000/yr → ~$2,400/yr.
