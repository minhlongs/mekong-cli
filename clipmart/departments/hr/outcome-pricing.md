# Outcome-Based Pricing Rationale — HR Department

## Market Reference Points

| Vendor | What They Charge | Annual Cost |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|
| HR Manager salary | $75,000-110,000/yr | $90,000/yr avg |
| Gusto (payroll+HR) | $40+$6/person/mo | $3,360/yr (20 ppl) |
| Lattice (performance) | $11/person/mo | $2,640/yr (20 ppl) |
| BambooHR | $8/person/mo | $1,920/yr (20 ppl) |
| Rippling | $8/person/mo | $1,920/yr (20 ppl) |

## Our Pricing Model

```
Base: $49/mo (200 credits)
Onboarding package: $15
Performance review cycle: $20
HR policy document: $12
Annual review coordination: $25
```

**Savings vs. HR Manager:** $90,000/yr → ~$1,200/yr for equivalent HR operations.
