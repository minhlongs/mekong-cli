## Our Pricing Model

```
Base: $49/mo (200 credits)
SOC2 readiness assessment: $80
SOX cycle run: $50
ITGC control test: $20
Audit trail report: $15
Compliance check (per control): $5
```

**Savings vs. Vanta + compliance officer:** $138,000/yr → ~$3,600/yr for equivalent coverage.
