# Outcome-Based Pricing Rationale — Strategy Department

## Market Reference Points

| Vendor | What They Charge | Annual Cost |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|
| McKinsey strategy engagement | $500k-2M/project | Variable |
| Fractional Chief of Staff | $5,000-15,000/mo | $60,000-180,000/yr |
| OKR software (Lattice) | $11/person/mo × 20 | $2,640/yr |
| Board management (Boardable) | $199-399/mo | $2,388-4,788/yr |
| Strategy consultant (boutique) | $250-500/hr | $30,000-60,000/yr |

## Our Pricing Model

```
Base: $49/mo (200 credits)
Annual OKR plan: $75
Quarterly board report: $45
SWOT + competitive analysis: $30
IPO readiness assessment: $150
```

**Savings vs. fractional Chief of Staff:** $5,000/mo → $49/mo floor.
For 4 strategic deliverables/month: $60 vs $5,000. 99% savings.

## Why Per-Deliverable

Strategy work is deliverable-driven: a plan, a report, an analysis. Pricing per deliverable makes the value measurable. You pay $75 for an OKR plan that a consultant would charge $5,000-15,000 to produce.
