# Outcome-Based Pricing Rationale — Fundraising Department

## Market Reference Points

| Vendor | What They Charge | Annual Cost |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|
| Fundraising advisor (placement) | 3-5% of raise | $45,000-75,000 on $1.5M |
| Fundraising advisor (retainer) | $10,000-20,000/mo | $120,000-240,000/yr |
| Pitch deck designer | $3,000-8,000/deck | Per project |
| Carta (cap table) | $3,600-9,600/yr | $3,600-9,600/yr |
| VC relationship platform | $5,000-15,000/yr | $5,000-15,000/yr |

## Our Pricing Model

```
Base: $49/mo (200 credits)
Pitch deck generation: $50
Investor outreach batch (25): $25
Cap table analysis: $20
Due diligence data room: $75
```

**Savings vs. placement fee:** $50 for a pitch deck vs. $3,000-8,000 designer. $25 for 25 personalized VC emails vs. advisor doing it for $2,000/week.
