# Outcome-Based Pricing Rationale — Communications Department

## Market Reference Points

| Vendor | What They Charge | Annual Cost |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|
| Comms Manager salary | $80,000-120,000/yr | $100,000/yr avg |
| PR Agency retainer | $5,000-15,000/mo | $60,000-180,000/yr |
| Press release distribution (PR Newswire) | $500-3,000/release | Per release |
| Mailchimp (internal comms) | $13-350/mo | $156-4,200/yr |
| Notion (docs) | $16/user/mo | $3,840/yr (20 users) |

## Our Pricing Model

```
Base: $49/mo (200 credits)
Press release draft: $20
Internal announcement: $8
Board minutes: $15
Crisis comms brief: $30
Executive post: $15
```

**Savings vs. PR agency retainer:** $10,000/mo → $49 floor + $100-300 for same output volume.
