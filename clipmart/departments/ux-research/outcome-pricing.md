# Outcome-Based Pricing Rationale — UX Research Department

## Market Reference Points

| Vendor | What They Charge | Annual Cost |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|
| UX Researcher (contract) | $150-250/hr | $86,400/yr (FT equiv) |
| UserTesting platform | $15,000-30,000/yr | $20,000/yr avg |
| Dovetail (research repo) | $30/user/mo | $3,600/yr |
| Maze (usability testing) | $75-1,250/mo | $900-15,000/yr |
| Ethnio (recruiting) | $900/mo | $10,800/yr |

## Our Pricing Model

```
Base: $49/mo (200 credits)
Usability test plan: $20
Interview synthesis (5): $25
UX audit report: $35
Design recommendations: $18
```

**Savings vs. contract researcher at $180/hr:** Our $35 UX audit vs. $180/hr × 5hrs = $900.
