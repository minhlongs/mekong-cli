# Outcome-Based Pricing Rationale — QA Engineering Department

## Market Reference Points

| Vendor | What They Charge | Annual Cost |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|
| QA Engineer | $100,000-140,000/yr | $120,000/yr avg |
| Playwright Cloud | $400/mo | $4,800/yr |
| k6 Cloud | $100-500/mo | $1,200-6,000/yr |
| Applitools (visual testing) | $12/user/mo | $1,440/yr |
| BrowserStack | $299-599/mo | $3,588-7,188/yr |

## Our Pricing Model

```
Base: $49/mo (200 credits)
E2E suite run: $5
Regression suite: $8
Performance benchmark: $12
Accessibility audit: $15
```

**Savings vs. QA engineer:** $120,000/yr → ~$1,800/yr for automated test runs on every release.
