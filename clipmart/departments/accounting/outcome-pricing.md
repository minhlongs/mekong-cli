# Outcome-Based Pricing Rationale — Accounting Department

## Why Per-Transaction Pricing?

Accounting is one of the most automatable labor markets. Every transaction follows rules. AI agents can categorize, reconcile, and close books with >99% accuracy. Per-transaction pricing aligns cost with volume.

**Traditional startup accounting cost:** $48,000-80,000/yr (bookkeeper + CPA firm)

## Our Pricing Model

```
Base: $49/mo (200 credits = 400 transactions)
Per daily bookkeeping run: $5
Per invoice batch (50): $10
Monthly close: $40
AR/AP aging report: $15
Annual tax prep package: $100
```

**Savings vs. Pilot:** $499/mo flat vs. our $49 floor + $0.50/transaction.
For 500 transactions/month: Pilot = $499, us = $49 + $250 = $299. 40% savings.

## Volume Tiers

| Monthly transactions | Price each | Effective monthly |
|--------------------|------------|-------------------|
| 1-400 | $0.50 | $49 floor |
| 401-2,000 | $0.40 | $160-800 |
| 2,001+ | $0.30 | $600+ |

## Outcome Definition

A "transaction" is one categorized + reconciled financial entry: invoice, payment, expense, journal entry. Monthly close and reports are flat-fee deliverables on top.
