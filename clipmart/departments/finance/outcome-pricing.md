# Outcome-Based Pricing Rationale — Finance Department

## Market Reference Points

| Vendor | What They Charge | Annual Cost |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|
| Fractional CFO | $5,000-15,000/mo | $60,000-180,000/yr |
| FP&A software (Mosaic) | $3,000-5,000/mo | $36,000-60,000/yr |
| Pilot (FP&A add-on) | $500-1,000/mo | $6,000-12,000/yr |
| Jirav (FP&A) | $1,200-2,500/mo | $14,400-30,000/yr |
| Accounting firm (close) | $2,000-5,000/mo | $24,000-60,000/yr |

## Our Pricing Model

```
Base: $49/mo (200 credits)
Monthly financial close: $50
Annual budget plan: $60
Collections workflow: $20
Investor financial report: $40
```

**Savings vs. fractional CFO:** $8,000/mo → ~$200/mo for equivalent deliverables.

## Why Per-Deliverable

Finance deliverables are discrete and measurable: a close, a budget, a forecast. Per-deliverable pricing ties cost to output, not headcount.
