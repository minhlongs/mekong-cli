# Outcome-Based Pricing Rationale — CX Support Department

## Market Reference Points

| Vendor | What They Charge | Annual Cost |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|
| Support Rep salary | $45,000-65,000/yr | $55,000/yr avg |
| Intercom (AI-assisted) | $74-374/mo | $888-4,488/yr |
| Zendesk Suite | $55-115/agent/mo | $6,600-13,800/yr (10 agents) |
| Freshdesk | $15-49/agent/mo | $1,800-5,880/yr (10 agents) |
| Fin AI by Intercom | $0.99/resolution | Per resolution |

## Our Pricing Model

```
Base: $49/mo (200 credits = 65 tickets)
L1 ticket resolved: $0.75
L2 ticket resolved: $1.50
KB article: $5
CSAT report: $8
```

**Savings vs. 2 support reps:** $110,000/yr → ~$3,600/yr for equivalent resolution volume.
Competitive with Fin AI ($0.99/resolution) at $0.75/ticket while including KB + CSAT.
