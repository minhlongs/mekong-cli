# Outcome-Based Pricing Rationale — Legal Department

## Why Per-Outcome Pricing?

Legal services have been priced at $300-600/hr for decades. AI agents can deliver the first 80% of routine legal work in minutes. Per-document pricing makes the value exchange transparent.

## Our Pricing Model

```
Base: $49/mo (200 credits)
Contract review + redlines: $25
Privacy policy draft: $20
Employment agreement: $30
Vendor NDA: $15
Board resolution: $20
```

**Savings vs. outside counsel:** A single NDA review costs $800-2,000 with a law firm. We charge $15. That's 98% savings on routine work.

## What This Does NOT Replace

This department is not a law firm and does not provide legal advice. It reduces attorney time by 80% for routine work. You still need an attorney to:
- Sign off on significant contracts
- Provide jurisdiction-specific legal advice
- Represent you in disputes or litigation
