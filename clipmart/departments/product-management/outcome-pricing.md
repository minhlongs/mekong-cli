# Outcome-Based Pricing Rationale — Product Management Department

## Market Reference Points

| Vendor | What They Charge | Annual Cost |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|
| Senior PM salary | $150,000-200,000/yr | $175,000/yr avg |
| ProductBoard | $20-80/creator/mo | $2,400-9,600/yr |
| Aha! Roadmaps | $59/user/mo | $7,080/yr |
| Linear | $8/user/mo × 10 | $960/yr |
| Competitor intel (Klue) | $5,000-15,000/yr | $10,000/yr avg |

## Our Pricing Model

```
Base: $49/mo (200 credits)
Sprint plan + tickets: $20
Feature launch checklist: $15
Competitive intelligence report: $25
Product retrospective: $12
```

**Savings vs. PM salary:** $175,000/yr → ~$2,400/yr for equivalent output.
