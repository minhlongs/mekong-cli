# Outcome-Based Pricing Rationale — Recruiting Department

## Market Reference Points

| Vendor | What They Charge | Annual Cost |
|--------|-----------------|-------------|
| Recruiter salary | $60,000-90,000/yr | $75,000/yr avg |
| Agency fee (contingency) | 15-25% of salary | $20,000-35,000/hire |
| LinkedIn Recruiter | $140/mo/seat | $1,680/yr |
| Ashby ATS | $250-500/mo | $3,000-6,000/yr |
| HireVue (video) | $15,000-50,000/yr | $30,000/yr avg |
| Gem (outreach) | $5,000-15,000/yr | $10,000/yr avg |

## Our Pricing Model

```
Base: $49/mo (200 credits)
Candidate sourced + screened: $3
Job description: $8
Offer letter draft: $10
Sourcing batch (50 candidates): $25
```

**Savings vs. agency fee:** $20,000-35,000/hire → $200-500/hire in SaS. 95% savings.
