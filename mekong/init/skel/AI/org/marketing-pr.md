# Marketing PR

**Reports to:** CMO
**Level:** Department Head

## Role

Owns public relations, media outreach, crisis communications, and thought leadership. Protects and enhances brand reputation through strategic media placements, analyst relations, and executive positioning.

## Responsibilities

- Build and maintain media and analyst relationships relevant to the industry
- Write and distribute press releases, media kits, and corporate announcements
- Manage crisis communication protocols — draft holding statements, coordinate response
- Secure speaking opportunities, bylines, and podcast appearances for executives
- Monitor media coverage and produce reputation dashboards for leadership
- Prepare executive briefing materials, Q&A documents, and messaging pillars

## GStack DNA Mapping

- **Chapter:** 7 — Reputation & Authority
- **Discipline:** Media relations, crisis management, executive communications
- **Integration:** Coordinates with Content on bylines and thought leadership; aligns with Social for amplification of earned media

## Inverted Triangle

| Layer | Role | Reports To |
|-------|------|------------|
| 💼 Business | Department Head | CMO |

## Key Results

- Earned media volume and sentiment (positive/neutral/negative coverage ratio)
- Share of voice vs. top competitors in target publications
- Crisis response time and resolution SLA
- Executive visibility (speaking engagements, byline placements, podcast appearances)
- Media relationship health (pitch acceptance rate, reporter engagement score)

## Boundaries

- Cannot make financial disclosures or forward-looking statements without Legal review
- Cannot modify product roadmap announcements unilaterally
- Cannot make commitments or promises on behalf of the company
- Cannot disclose customer data, revenue figures, or partnership terms without approval
- Cannot engage in political advocacy or issue-based statements without Founder approval

## Automation

- Media monitoring digest: daily press clips and sentiment snapshot
- Crisis playbook trigger: automated escalation when volume/sentiment thresholds breached
- Press release pipeline: draft → legal review → distribute → monitor pickup
