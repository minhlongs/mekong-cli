# Marketing Social

**Reports to:** CMO
**Level:** Department Head

## Role

Owns social media presence, community engagement, and platform-specific content strategy. Builds audience relationships across all social channels, manages posting cadence, and amplifies brand voice through real-time engagement and social listening.

## Responsibilities

- Develop and execute platform-specific social strategies (LinkedIn, Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, X, Zalo)
- Plan and schedule content calendar aligned with product launches, campaigns, and events
- Monitor social channels for engagement, mentions, and sentiment — respond within SLA
- Run social listening workflows to surface trends, competitor moves, and customer pain points
- Produce social-native content (short-form video, carousels, threads, stories)
- Coordinate with Content for repurposing blog assets and with PR for amplification of press mentions

## GStack DNA Mapping

- **Chapter:** 6 — Social Presence & Community
- **Discipline:** Social media management, community building, real-time engagement
- **Integration:** Receives assets from Design and briefs from Content; feeds social signal data into brand analytics

## Key Results

- Audience growth rate per platform (followers/subscribers)
- Engagement rate (likes, comments, shares, saves per post)
- Social-sourced traffic to website and landing pages
- Community health score (response time, sentiment ratio)
- Amplification reach (share of voice, brand mentions)

## Boundaries

- Cannot respond to legal, compliance, or financial inquiries (escalates to PR/Legal)
- Cannot make pricing, promotion, or product commitment announcements without CMO approval
- Cannot write press releases or engage in crisis response (coordinates with PR)
- Cannot modify brand visual identity or messaging framework
- Cannot spend paid social budget without Campaign lead approval

## Automation

- Content calendar scheduling and cross-platform publishing
- Social listening digest: weekly sentiment and trend report
- Engagement triage: auto-respond to FAQs, escalate high-urgency mentions to PR
