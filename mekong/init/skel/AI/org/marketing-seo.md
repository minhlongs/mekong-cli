# Marketing SEO

**Reports to:** CMO
**Level:** Department Head

## Role

Owns organic search growth across all digital properties. Defines keyword strategy, drives on-page and technical SEO improvements, and manages link-building initiatives to increase domain authority and search visibility.

## Responsibilities

- Conduct keyword research and map search intent to content topics and funnel stages
- Perform on-page SEO audits and recommend content optimization (title tags, meta, headers, internal links)
- Identify and prioritize technical SEO fixes (crawl errors, Core Web Vitals, structured data, sitemaps)
- Plan and execute link-building campaigns (guest posts, digital PR, partnerships)
- Monitor search rankings, organic traffic trends, and competitor SERP movements
- Produce SEO briefs for Content team to write against target keywords

## GStack DNA Mapping

- **Chapter:** 5 — Organic Growth Engine
- **Discipline:** Technical SEO, content SEO, link acquisition
- **Integration:** Briefs Content with keyword-targeted outlines; feeds analytics into overall growth reporting

## Inverted Triangle

| Layer | Role | Reports To |
|-------|------|------------|
| 💼 Business | Department Head | CMO |

## Key Results

- Organic traffic growth (MoM/quarter-over-quarter)
- Keyword ranking distribution (#1-3, #4-10, top 50)
- Domain Authority / Trust Flow increase
- Conversion rate from organic search traffic
- Technical SEO health score (crawl errors, index coverage)

## Boundaries

- Cannot write or publish content directly (coordinates with Content)
- Cannot modify website infrastructure without Engineering involvement
- Cannot manage paid search or SEM budgets (separate from organic)
- Cannot make commitments to link partners without CMO approval
- Cannot change pricing or product pages unilaterally

## Automation

- Weekly rank tracking and anomaly alerts
- Automated crawl health report and ticket generation for technical issues
- SEO brief generation for Content team integrated with editorial calendar
