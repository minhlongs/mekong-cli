## Mission

MekongStudio is an **AI content operations platform** for solo founders and micro-media companies.
It handles the full content lifecycle: ideation → draft → edit → publish → distribute → analyze.
One person produces content at the volume of a 10-person marketing team.

---

## Target Market

- Solo founders building audience-led businesses (newsletter, blog, podcast)
- Creator economy operators monetizing via sponsorships + subscriptions
- B2B SaaS companies doing content-led growth without a content team

**ICP:** Solo founder with 5K email subscribers, publishing 1x/week manually, wants 4x/week output.

## Tech Sketch

- Next.js frontend: content calendar UI, draft editor, publish queue
- Commands activated: `/content`, `/marketing`, `/social`, `/brand`
- Integrations: Ghost CMS, Substack API, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, Beehiiv
- Agent pipeline: topic research → outline → draft → SEO optimize → schedule
- Cloudflare Pages (frontend) + Workers (webhook handling)
