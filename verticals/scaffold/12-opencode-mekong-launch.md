## Mission

MekongLaunch is an **AI-powered growth and distribution engine** that orchestrates product launches
and ongoing distribution across all channels. It plans, schedules, executes, and analyzes
launch campaigns — giving a solo founder the distribution power of a full growth team.

---

## Target Market

- Solo founders launching their first or next SaaS product
- Indie hackers who build great products but struggle with distribution
- Micro-ISVs looking to break through noise with coordinated multi-channel launches

**ICP:** Solo founder with a working product, zero distribution strategy, 30 days to first revenue.

## Tech Sketch

- OpenCode integration: auto-generate open-source repo assets (README, CONTRIBUTING, demos) for distribution
- Commands activated: `/marketing`, `/social`, `/pitch`, `/producthunt`, `/launch` (new)
- Channel orchestration: Product Hunt, Hacker News Show HN, Reddit, LinkedIn, Twitter/X, newsletter
- AI copywriter: generates channel-specific copy variants per audience segment
- Analytics: tracks launch metrics across channels, surfaces winning messages
