## Value Proposition

### Core Message

> Cut 40-60 hours/month of admin work with AI automation built for manufacturers.

### ROI Calculation

```
Labor Savings:     58 hrs × $75/hr  = $4,350/month
Waste Reduction:   Inventory 12%→4% = $4,051/month
Subscription:      $149/mo (Pro)    = -$149/month
─────────────────────────────────────────────────
Net Value:                          = $8,252/month
ROI:                                = 57x
```

### Manufacturing Pain Points

| Pain Point | Impact | OpenClaw Solution |
|------------|--------|-------------------|
| Spreadsheets for inventory | 15-20 hrs/week admin | Automated tracking + expiry alerts |
| WhatsApp/Zalo for orders | Missing orders, no audit trail | CRM portal with order status |
| Manual quality checks | Inconsistent, time-consuming | AI-generated reports |
| Gut-feel scheduling | Missed deadlines | Capacity-based planning |
| Paper downtime logs | No root cause analysis | Digital tracking + analytics |
| No batch traceability | 3-day audit prep → 45 min | Full audit trail auto-generated |

## Target Persona

### Primary: Manufacturing SME Owner

- **Age:** 40-60
- **Company:** 20-200 employees, $5M-$50M revenue
- **Mindset:** Pragmatic, ROI-focused, skeptical of "AI hype"

- **Goal:** Margin improvement, scalability, sellable business

### Secondary: Operations Director

- **Age:** 35-50
- **Role:** Hands-on, drowning in daily fires
- **Pain:** No visibility, constant expediting, audit stress
- **Goal:** Automation, predictability, weekends back
- **Buying Signal:** New ownership pressure, capacity constraints

## Appendix C: Objection Handling Scripts

### Price Objections

**"Too expensive for us right now"**

> I hear you. Can I ask — what's "expensive" relative to?
>
> Because if we're comparing $149/mo to $8,400/month in value... that's 57x ROI.
>
> What's 58 hours of your ops manager's time worth? At $75/hr, that's $4,350. Plus ~$4,000 in waste reduction.
>
> So the question isn't "Can we afford $149?" It's "Can we afford NOT to do this?"
>
> That said — I get it. Budget cycles are real. When does your next planning cycle start?

### Timing Objections

**"Call me next quarter"**

> Happy to. What specifically changes next quarter?
>
> [Listen]
>
> Got it. Here's what I'd suggest: Let's do a quick 15-min demo now, I'll show you the ROI calculator, and you can use that internally when budget comes up.
>
> We also have founder pricing for our 5 pilot slots — after that, pricing goes up.
>
> Worth exploring?

### Authority Objections

**"I need to talk to [partner/board/CEO]"**

> Smart move. Who else should be part of the conversation?
>
> [Listen]
>
> Here's what I'd suggest: Let's do a 15-min demo together with [them]. I can walk through the ROI live and answer any questions.
>
> Does [Thursday at 2pm] or [Friday at 10am] work?

### Competitor Objections

**"We're already using [competitor/software]"**

> That's great — what are you using specifically?
>
> [Listen]
>
> Interesting. Most of our customers came from [that solution]. What they found was [differentiator].
>
> Would it make sense to compare notes? 15 minutes, no pressure.
