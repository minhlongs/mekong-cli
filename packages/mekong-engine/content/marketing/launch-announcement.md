## Body

**SAN FRANCISCO, CA** — OpenClaw today announced the launch of its RaaS (Robot-as-a-Service) Gateway, a revolutionary platform that enables SaaS companies to deploy AI agents at the edge with unprecedented speed and cost efficiency.

### The Problem

Deploying AI agents at scale requires significant infrastructure investment, complex billing systems, and dedicated DevOps teams. Most SaaS companies struggle with:
- High cloud costs (50-80% wasted on idle capacity)
- Complex multi-tenant authentication
- Unpredictable AI API costs
- Slow deployment cycles (weeks to months)

### The Solution

OpenClaw RaaS Gateway provides:
- ⚡ **Instant deployment** — From zero to production in 5 minutes
- 💰 **Metered billing** — Pay-per-use MCU model (no idle costs)
- 🌍 **Edge-native** — Cloudflare Workers deployment (50ms p95 latency)
- 🔐 **Enterprise security** — Multi-tenant auth, rate limiting, audit logs
- 📊 **Real-time analytics** — Usage dashboard + Prometheus metrics
