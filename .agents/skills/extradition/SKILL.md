# /extradition — Extradition — Alias for Vietnamese Mutual Legal Assistance & Extradition Suite (Luật Tương trợ tư pháp 2007)

Extradition — Alias for Vietnamese Mutual Legal Assistance & Extradition Suite (Luật Tương trợ tư pháp 2007).

## Usage

```bash
// turbo
mekong extradition $ARGUMENTS
```

## Subcommands

| Subcommand | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| `civil` | Submit and record a Civil Mutual Legal Assistance Request under Art 10-16. |
| `criminal` | Submit and record a Criminal Mutual Legal Assistance Request under Art 17-31. |
| `extradition` | Evaluate and record an Extradition Dossier according to statutory standards (Art 32-48). |
| `transfer` | Process and record the Transfer of a Sentenced Person under Art 49-64. |
| `treaty` | Register a Bilateral Mutual Legal Assistance / Extradition Treaty. |
| `list` | List cross-border judicial assistance records by category. |
| `status` | Show cross-border judicial assistance telemetry and system status. |
