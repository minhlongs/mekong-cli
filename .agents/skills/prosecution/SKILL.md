# /prosecution — Prosecution — Vietnamese People's Procuracy, Public Prosecution & Judicial Supervision Suite (Luật Tổ chức VKSND 2014 & BLTTHS 2015)

Prosecution — Vietnamese People's Procuracy, Public Prosecution & Judicial Supervision Suite (Luật Tổ chức VKSND 2014 & BLTTHS 2015).

## Usage

```bash
// turbo
mekong prosecution $ARGUMENTS
```

## Subcommands

| Subcommand | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| `procurator` | Register a Procurator under the Law on Organization of the People's Procuracies 2014. |
| `report` | Record and supervise receipt of crime reports and denunciations under Articles 144-150 BLTTHS 2015. |
| `case` | Record criminal case investigation supervision and procedural approvals. |
| `indictment` | Issue a formal prosecutorial indictment (Cáo trạng) under Articles 243-244 BLTTHS 2015. |
| `inspection` | Record inspection of detention facility and prison execution under Law on Execution of Criminal Judgments 2019. |
| `list` | List records from the People's Procuracy and Public Prosecution database. |
| `status` | Display telemetry metrics for the People's Procuracy and Public Prosecution Suite. |
