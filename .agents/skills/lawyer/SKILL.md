# /lawyer — Lawyer — Vietnamese Legal Profession, Bar Association & Law Practice Suite (Luật Luật sư 2006/2012)

Lawyer — Vietnamese Legal Profession, Bar Association & Law Practice Suite (Luật Luật sư 2006/2012).

## Usage

```bash
// turbo
mekong lawyer $ARGUMENTS
```

## Subcommands

| Subcommand | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| `attorney` | Register or update a practicing lawyer admitted to the Bar under Law on Lawyers. |
| `firm` | Register or update a Law Practice Organization licensed by Department of Justice. |
| `contract` | Execute a mandatory statutory Legal Service Contract under Articles 54-56 Law on Lawyers. |
| `defense` | Record formal participation in court or investigation proceedings (Thông báo người bào chữa). |
| `ethics` | Perform professional ethics, conflict of interest, and mandatory pro bono hours audit. |
| `list` | List records by category (lawyers, firms, contracts, litigation, ethics). |
| `status` | Display bar telemetry and legal practice operational status. |
