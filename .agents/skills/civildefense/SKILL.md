# /civildefense — Civil Defense — Vietnamese Civil Defense, Disaster Mitigation & Emergency Response Suite (Luật Phòng thủ dân sự 2023)

Civil Defense — Vietnamese Civil Defense, Disaster Mitigation & Emergency Response Suite (Luật Phòng thủ dân sự 2023).

## Usage

```bash
// turbo
mekong civildefense $ARGUMENTS
```

## Subcommands

| Subcommand | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| `plan` | Register or update a civil defense readiness plan (Article 13 Law 18/2023/QH15). |
| `alert` | Issue or escalate a civil defense alert level (Article 20 Law 18/2023/QH15). |
| `shelter` | Register and certify a civil defense shelter or bunker (Article 27 Law 18/2023/QH15). |
| `force` | Mobilize and deploy specialized civil defense response forces (Article 35 Law 18/2023/QH15). |
| `drill` | Record civil defense emergency drills and preparedness exercises (Article 18 Law 18/2023/QH15). |
| `list` | List civil defense plans, alerts, shelters, forces, and emergency drills. |
| `status` | Get aggregate telemetry metrics on national civil defense readiness and response posture. |
