# /identity — Identity — Vietnamese National Identification, Electronic Identity (VNeID) & Biometrics Suite (Luật Căn cước 2023 & NĐ 69/2024/NĐ-CP)

Identity — Vietnamese National Identification, Electronic Identity (VNeID) & Biometrics Suite (Luật Căn cước 2023 & NĐ 69/2024/NĐ-CP).

## Usage

```bash
// turbo
mekong identity $ARGUMENTS
```

## Subcommands

| Subcommand | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| `card` | Issue or register an Identity Card (Thẻ Căn cước) under the Law on Identification 2023. |
| `vneid` | Provision or update an Electronic Identity (VNeID) account under Decree 69/2024/NĐ-CP. |
| `biometric` | Enroll biometric data (Iris, Face, Fingerprint, DNA, Voice) under Articles 15 & 16 Law 26/2023/QH15. |
| `cert` | Issue an Identity Certificate (Giấy chứng nhận căn cước) to a person of Vietnamese origin without nationality. |
| `verify` | Verify identity card or certificate against the National Database under Decree 69/2024/NĐ-CP. |
| `list` | List records from the National Identification database. |
| `status` | Display telemetry metrics for National Identity, VNeID, and Biometrics databases. |
