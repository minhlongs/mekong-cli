# /adminlaw — AdminLaw — Vietnamese Legal Normative Documents, RIA & State Compensation Liability Suite (Luật VBQPPL & Luật TNBTNN)

AdminLaw — Vietnamese Legal Normative Documents, RIA & State Compensation Liability Suite (Luật VBQPPL & Luật TNBTNN).

## Usage

```bash
// turbo
mekong adminlaw $ARGUMENTS
```

## Subcommands

| Subcommand | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| `document` | Register or update a Vietnamese legal normative document under Article 4 Law No. 80/2015/QH13. |
| `ria` | Conduct Regulatory Impact Assessment (RIA) & legality appraisal under Articles 35 & 58 Law No. 80/2015/QH13. |
| `claim` | File a State Compensation liability claim under Articles 2 & 41-43 Law No. 10/2017/QH14. |
| `settle` | Issue a State Compensation settlement decision under Articles 45-48 Law No. 10/2017/QH14. |
| `reimburse` | Order at-fault state officer reimbursement to State Budget under Articles 64-67 Law No. 10/2017/QH14. |
| `list` | List records from administrative law database by category. |
| `status` | Display Administrative Law, RIA & State Compensation status telemetry. |
