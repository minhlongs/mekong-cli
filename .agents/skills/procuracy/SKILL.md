# /procuracy — Procuracy — Vietnamese People's Procuracy, Public Prosecution & Judicial Supervision Suite (Luật Tổ chức VKSND 2014)

Procuracy — Vietnamese People's Procuracy, Public Prosecution & Judicial Supervision Suite (Luật Tổ chức VKSND 2014).

## Usage

```bash
// turbo
mekong procuracy $ARGUMENTS
```

## Subcommands

| Subcommand | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| `prosecutor` | Register or update a Procurator (Kiểm sát viên) under Article 74 Law on Organization of People's Procuracies. |
| `indictment` | Issue or track a criminal prosecution indictment under Article 243 Criminal Procedure Code. |
| `detention` | Supervise legality of arrest, custody, and temporary detention under Articles 22-26 Law on Organization of People's Procuracies. |
| `protest` | Issue an appellate, cassation, or reopening protest against court judgment under Articles 27-31. |
| `list` | List records from People's Procuracy database by category. |
| `status` | Display People's Procuracy & Public Prosecution telemetry status. |
