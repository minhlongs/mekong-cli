# /anticorruption — Anti-Corruption — Vietnamese Anti-Corruption, Asset Declaration & Integrity Oversight Suite (Luật Phòng, chống tham nhũng 2018)

Anti-Corruption — Vietnamese Anti-Corruption, Asset Declaration & Integrity Oversight Suite (Luật Phòng, chống tham nhũng 2018).

## Usage

```bash
// turbo
mekong anticorruption $ARGUMENTS
```

## Subcommands

| Subcommand | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| `declare` | Register a formal Asset and Income Declaration pursuant to Decree 130/2020/NĐ-CP. |
| `verify` | Execute an integrity verification audit and determine unexplained wealth under Decree 130/2020/NĐ-CP. |
| `gift` | Record official gift surrender and treasury receipt under Article 22 of Law 36/2018/QH14. |
| `conflict` | Register or resolve conflict of interest under Articles 23 & 29 of Law 36/2018/QH14. |
| `sanction` | Record disciplinary sanction or criminal referral under Article 51 of Law 36/2018/QH14. |
| `list` | List asset declarations, verifications, gifts, conflicts, and sanctions. |
| `status` | Get aggregate telemetry metrics on anti-corruption oversight and asset declarations. |
