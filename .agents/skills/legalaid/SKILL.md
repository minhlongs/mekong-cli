# /legalaid — LegalAid — Vietnamese State Legal Aid, Vulnerable Population Representation & Justice Access Suite (Luật Trợ giúp pháp lý 2017)

LegalAid — Vietnamese State Legal Aid, Vulnerable Population Representation & Justice Access Suite (Luật Trợ giúp pháp lý 2017).

## Usage

```bash
// turbo
mekong legalaid $ARGUMENTS
```

## Subcommands

| Subcommand | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| `beneficiary` | Register or update an eligible legal aid beneficiary under Article 7 Law on Legal Aid. |
| `officer` | Register or update a State Legal Aid Officer or contracted lawyer under Articles 17-23. |
| `request` | File or update a legal aid application/case docket under Articles 29-33. |
| `proceeding` | Issue appointment decision for legal aid officer to participate in proceedings under Article 31. |
| `eval` | Evaluate case quality under Circular No. 08/2017/TT-BTP (Standard, Good, Excellent, Fail). |
| `list` | List legal aid records by category (beneficiaries, officers, requests, proceedings, evaluations). |
| `status` | Display State Legal Aid telemetry and vulnerable population representation operational status. |
