# /civilstatus — CivilStatus — Vietnamese Civil Status, Vital Statistics & Population Registration Suite (Luật Hộ tịch 2014 & Nghị định 123/2015/NĐ-CP)

CivilStatus — Vietnamese Civil Status, Vital Statistics & Population Registration Suite (Luật Hộ tịch 2014 & Nghị định 123/2015/NĐ-CP).

## Usage

```bash
// turbo
mekong civilstatus $ARGUMENTS
```

## Subcommands

| Subcommand | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| `birth` | Register birth and assign personal identification number (DDCN) under Law on Civil Status 2014. |
| `marriage` | Register civil marriage under Article 17-18 or Article 37-38 (Foreign element). |
| `death` | Register death and issue death certificate under Article 32-34. |
| `rectify` | Rectify, change, or correct civil status record under Article 26-28. |
| `extract` | Issue verified digital extract of civil status record under Decree 87/2020/ND-CP. |
| `list` | List civil status and vital statistics records by category. |
| `status` | Show Vietnamese civil status and vital statistics telemetry. |
