# /coastguard — Coast Guard — Vietnamese Coast Guard & Maritime Law Enforcement Suite (Luật Cảnh sát biển Việt Nam 2018)

Coast Guard — Vietnamese Coast Guard & Maritime Law Enforcement Suite (Luật Cảnh sát biển Việt Nam 2018).

## Usage

```bash
// turbo
mekong coastguard $ARGUMENTS
```

## Subcommands

| Subcommand | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| `vessel` | Register a Coast Guard patrol cutter or specialized ship (Article 29 Law 33/2018/QH14). |
| `patrol` | Log a maritime sovereignty patrol or EEZ surveillance sortie (Article 11 Law 33/2018/QH14). |
| `inspect` | Record a boarding inspection and maritime law enforcement interdiction (Article 13 Law 33/2018/QH14). |
| `iuu` | Record an IUU fishing infringement and statutory sanction (Decree 42/2019/NĐ-CP & EC Yellow Card Action). |
| `sar` | Log a maritime search and rescue or maritime disaster relief sortie (Article 8 Law 33/2018/QH14). |
| `list` | List Coast Guard vessels, patrols, boarding inspections, IUU crackdowns, and SAR missions. |
| `status` | Get aggregate telemetry metrics on Coast Guard fleet readiness, patrols, and maritime law enforcement. |
