## 1. Demonstrable User Journey for One Zalo OA Customer

1. **Customer Inquiry on Zalo OA (`T + 0s`):**
   - A Vietnamese buyer messages the shop's Zalo Official Account (e.g., *"Shop báo giá gói tư vấn AI và tích hợp VietQR 10 giờ, xuất hóa đơn VAT 8% nhé"*).
2. **Webhook Intake, Quarantine & Deterministic Quote (`T + 11–15ms` synthetic / `< 1.5s` with local MLX):**
   - FastAPI `POST /v1/opc/pilot-a/webhook/zalo` verifies `X-ZEvent-Mac` (`sha256(app_id + raw_body + timestamp + secret)`), checks the 48-hour Zalo customer interaction window and Decree 13 consent, runs `QuarantineFloor` prompt-injection screening, redacts Vietnamese PII (phone, MST, CCCD, bank account), extracts `source_span` citations via Profile 4 `SelectiveRouter`, computes exact `Decimal` line-item totals + dynamic EMVCo CRC16-CCITT VietQR (`OPC run_<id>`), and pauses `GovernedStateGraph` at `INTERRUPT_AUTHORIZE`.
   - If extraction confidence `< 0.85` or math validation fails, the system halts auto-quoting and routes immediately to `HANDOFF_ZALO_OA_MANAGER`.
3. **1-Tap Shop Owner Approval (`10–30s` human review):**
   - The shop owner opens either the `<15KB` Vietnamese mobile approval card (`/v1/opc/pilot-a/card/{run_id}` protected by `X-Card-Sig`, `no-store`, `no-referrer`, strict CSP, and URL `sig` scrubbing) or the Dashboard OPC Cockpit (`/dashboard/opc` backed by the `HttpOnly` HMAC-SHA256 BFF `/api/opc/pilot-a/*`).
   - Owner reviews the highlighted `source_span` evidence, line items, and total VND, then taps **Duyệt & Gửi Báo Giá + VietQR** (or **Chuyển Nhân Viên Chat Tay** / **Yêu cầu sửa**).
   - Gateway verifies anti-TOCTOU `payload_sha256`, consumes the single-use approval token atomically in SQLite WAL, re-checks the live 48h Zalo window, and dispatches the quote + VietQR once (`opc_side_effect_journal` prevents duplicates).
4. **Instant Bank Transfer & Pre-Accounting Draft (`T + customer payment`):**
   - Customer scans the dynamic Napas 247 VietQR and pays into the shop owner's own bank account (money goes directly to the merchant's bank account — Mekong CLI is non-custodial for merchant sales).
   - Signed bank webhook (`POST /v1/opc/pilot-a/webhook/vietqr` with `X-VietQR-Signature`) reconciles `bank_tx_id` 1:1 against `run_id` and `expected_amount_vnd`, producing an uncertified **`DRAFT_PENDING_TVAN`** file (`CSV`, `XML`, or `JSON`) ready for the owner's accountant to import into **MISA meInvoice** or **Sapo Invoice** for external TVAN digital signing.

4. **Dashboard BFF Login & Dry-Run Rehearsal (`5–10 minutes`):** Add `<slug>: mk_opc_*` to server-only `MEKONG_TENANT_KEYS_JSON`, sign in via `/dashboard/opc`, and approve 1 internal founder test message end-to-end.

## 4. Mandatory Human Approval Checkpoints

1. **Outbound Quote & Price Commitment (`INTERRUPT_AUTHORIZE`):** Zero quotes or VietQR payment requests can be sent to a Zalo customer without explicit owner `approve` bound to `payload_sha256`.
2. **Low-Confidence / Hallucination / Math Mismatch (`HANDOFF_ZALO_OA_MANAGER`):** Automatic hard halt whenever confidence `< 0.85`, unpriced bespoke items appear, or `Decimal` line math mismatches.
3. **Official Tax E-Invoice Issuance (`DRAFT_PENDING_TVAN`):** Mekong CLI exports **only** pre-accounting drafts (`legal_certified=False`, `requires_tvan_signature=True`). Final legal e-invoice signing and GDT tax code transmission happen exclusively inside the merchant's licensed MISA meInvoice / Sapo TVAN account by their accountant/owner.

## 7. Pilot Cohort Entry Criteria (Strict Filter — Who Qualifies for Customer #1)

Accept **only** Vietnamese SMEs meeting all 5 criteria:
1. Already holds an active, verified **Zalo Official Account on a paid package (`Dùng thử` / `Nâng cao` / `Premium`)** supporting Webhook + CS API.
2. Sells **standardized or semi-standardized B2B/B2C catalog items/services** (`5–50` SKUs with deterministic unit prices in VND, e.g., printing/packaging, interior workshop, wholesale materials, agency/IT services, equipment rental).
3. Collects payment via **domestic Napas 247 bank transfer** (MB, VCB, TCB, ACB, VietinBank, BIDV, VPBank, TPBank) and uses Sepay/Casso or bank webhook notifications.
4. Has **1 designated Shop Owner** willing to approve quotes on mobile/dashboard and **1 bookkeeper/accountant** using MISA meInvoice or Sapo Invoice (understanding Mekong exports drafts `DRAFT_PENDING_TVAN`, not self-signed tax invoices).
5. **Exclude** for Cohort 1: regulated medical/pharmacy advice, consumer lending/crypto, custom construction bidding with `>100` unpriced line items, or merchants expecting fully autonomous zero-human-approval outbound pricing.

## 9. Monitoring, Kill Switch, Rollback & Incident Response

- **Key Health & Security Signals to Watch:**
  - HTTP `401`/`403`/`409`/`429`/`503` rates on `/v1/opc/pilot-a/*` and `/v1/checkout/*`.
  - `WorkflowStatus.RECONCILIATION_REQUIRED` count (`opc_runs`) — alerts owner immediately when Zalo dispatch timed out so no blind retry occurs.
  - `UsageStage.UNKNOWN` (`has_unknown_costs=True`) and `WorkflowQuotaExceededError` (`HTTP 429`).
- **30-Second Emergency Kill Switches (No Code Deploy Needed):**
  1. **Disable Outbound Zalo Sending Immediately:** Unset/rotate `ZALO_OA_ACCESS_TOKEN` or switch cockpit operator to **Chuyển Nhân Viên Chat Tay (`handoff`)** — inbound inquiries still log safely or hand off to Zalo OA Manager without sending automated replies.
  2. **Revoke Dashboard Sessions Immediately:** Rotate `MEKONG_DASHBOARD_SESSION_SECRET` (`>= 32` bytes) and restart Dashboard — invalidates all active `mk_opc_session` cookies instantly.
  3. **Revoke Single Tenant Gateway Access:** Delete or rotate `tenant_api_key_sha256` in `<slug>.tenant.json` and `MEKONG_TENANT_KEYS_JSON`.
- **SQLite WAL Backup & Rollback:**
  - Run hourly `sqlite3 <db> ".backup '<snapshot>'"` on `<slug>.db`, `<slug>_vietqr.db`, `<slug>_privacy.db`, and `vietqr.db`.
  - Application rollback: redeploy previous container tag; `checkout_orders` and `opc_graph_checkpoints` migrations are strictly additive (`CREATE TABLE IF NOT EXISTS`).
- **72-Hour Breach / Incident Protocol (Decree 13 Article 26):** Isolate tenant key -> snapshot forensic SQLite WAL files -> assess PII exposure via `PrivacyEngine` -> notify affected merchant immediately and prepare A05 report within `< 72h` if personal data was exposed.
