## Pipeline Overview

```
Prospecting → Qualified → Demo → Pilot → Negotiation → Closed Won/Lost
    ↓            ↓         ↓       ↓         ↓             ↓
   100%        60%       40%     25%       15%           8%
```

**Average Sales Cycle:**
- Prop Shops: 14-30 days
- Family Offices: 30-60 days
- HNW Traders: 7-14 days
- Enterprise: 60-120 days

---

## Stage 0: Prospecting (Unqualified Leads)

**Definition:** Lead added to CRM, no contact yet

**Entry Criteria:**
- Company matches ICP firmographics
- Contact information available (email/LinkedIn)
- Lead score ≥ 40

**Exit Criteria (to Stage 1):**
- Initial outreach sent
- Response received (reply, call booked, or demo request)

**Activities:**
- Lead research and scoring
- Personalization research (LinkedIn, news, signals)
- Draft customized outreach

**Conversion Rate:** 100% → 60%

**Time in Stage:** 1-3 days

---

## Stage 1: Qualified (Discovery Call Booked)

**Definition:** Lead responded, discovery call scheduled

**Entry Criteria:**
- Lead replied to outreach OR booked demo
- Confirmed interest in algorithmic trading solution

**Exit Criteria (to Stage 2):**
- Discovery call completed
- Pain points confirmed
- Budget/timeline discussed
- Technical fit validated

**Activities:**
- Discovery call (15-30 min)
- Needs assessment questionnaire
- Current stack evaluation
- ROI calculation preview

**Key Questions:**
1. "What's your current trading setup?"
2. "What strategies are you running?"
3. "What's your monthly infra/tooling spend?"
4. "What would a 10x improvement look like?"
5. "Who else is involved in this decision?"

**Conversion Rate:** 60% → 40%

**Time in Stage:** 3-7 days

---

## Stage 2: Demo (Product Demonstration)

**Definition:** Live product demo scheduled and completed

**Entry Criteria:**
- Discovery call completed successfully
- Prospect expressed interest in seeing product

**Exit Criteria (to Stage 3):**
- Demo completed
- Prospect engaged (asked questions, requested features)
- Pilot proposal accepted

**Activities:**
- Personalized demo (use their exchange/strategy if possible)
- Show backtest results relevant to their segment
- Address technical objections
- Propose pilot terms

**Demo Agenda (30-45 min):**
1. Intro + their pain points (5 min)
2. Platform overview (10 min)
3. Live strategy demo (10 min)
4. Backtest results (5 min)
5. Q&A + next steps (10 min)

**Conversion Rate:** 40% → 25%

**Time in Stage:** 5-10 days

---

## Stage 3: Pilot (Paper Trading Trial)

**Definition:** Prospect running paper trading or limited live pilot

**Entry Criteria:**
- Demo completed successfully
- Prospect agreed to trial
- Pilot agreement signed (if required)

**Exit Criteria (to Stage 4):**
- Pilot completed (2-4 weeks)
- Positive results achieved
- Decision to proceed confirmed

**Activities:**
- Pilot onboarding (API keys, strategy selection)
- Weekly check-in calls
- Performance report sharing
- Address technical issues
- Build business case for paid tier

**Pilot Success Metrics:**
- Bot uptime > 99%
- Strategy executes as expected
- Prospect can navigate dashboard
- Positive P&L (or confident in backtest correlation)

**Conversion Rate:** 25% → 15%

**Time in Stage:** 14-28 days

---

## Stage 4: Negotiation (Commercial Discussion)

**Definition:** Pilot successful, negotiating terms

**Entry Criteria:**
- Pilot completed with positive results
- Prospect confirmed intent to purchase
- Budget holder engaged

**Exit Criteria (to Stage 5):**
- Pricing agreed
- Contract signed
- Payment processed

**Activities:**
- Proposal sent (pricing + terms)
- Negotiation calls
- Legal/security review (for enterprise)
- Procurement process navigation

**Common Objections + Responses:**

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Too expensive" | "At $149/mo, you need 0.5% monthly return to break even. Our pilot showed X%." |
| "Need to think about it" | "What specific concerns do you have? Let's address them now." |
| "Building in-house" | "Sure — that's $500K and 6 months. We're $149/mo and deployable today." |
| "Need more features" | "What's critical vs. nice-to-have? Roadmap alignment discussion." |
| "Competitor is cheaper" | "What's included? 1216 tests, 0 tech debt, 10+ strategies, 24/7 support?" |

**Conversion Rate:** 15% → 8%

**Time in Stage:** 7-21 days

---

## Stage 5: Closed Won

**Definition:** Deal signed, payment received

**Entry Criteria:**
- Contract signed
- Payment processed

**Activities:**
- Onboarding call scheduled
- API keys configured
- Strategy deployment assistance
- Success manager assigned

**Handoff to Customer Success:**
- Account documentation
- Usage patterns from pilot
- Expansion opportunities noted
- Renewal date set

**Win Rate by Segment:**

| Segment | Win Rate | Avg Deal Size |
|---------|----------|---------------|
| Prop Shops | 12% | $2,400/year |
| Family Offices | 8% | $12,000/year |
| HNW Traders | 25% | $600/year |
| Enterprise | 5% | $60,000/year |
| Partnerships | 15% | Revenue share |

---

## Stage 6: Closed Lost

**Definition:** Prospect decided not to proceed

**Exit Criteria:**
- Prospect explicitly declined
- 30+ days no response after pilot
- Chose competitor
- Budget cut/frozen

**Activities:**
- Exit survey: "Why did you decide not to proceed?"
- Add to nurture sequence (monthly newsletter)
- Re-engage in 90 days with new case studies

**Loss Reasons (Track in CRM):**

| Reason | % of Losses | Action |
|--------|-------------|--------|
| Price too high | 25% | Improve ROI demonstration |
| Building in-house | 20% | TCO comparison content |
| Not ready to automate | 15% | Nurture sequence |
| Competitor | 15% | Competitive battlecards |
| Regulatory concerns | 10% | Compliance documentation |
| Missing features | 10% | Product feedback loop |
| Other | 5% | — |

## Sales Velocity Formula

```
Sales Velocity = (Leads × Conv Rate × Avg Deal Size) / Sales Cycle Length

Example (Monthly):
- 200 leads entered
- 10% conversion = 20 customers
- $200 avg monthly deal = $4,000 MRR added
- 30-day cycle = same month recognition

Goal: Increase velocity by:
1. More leads (top of funnel)
2. Better conversion (sales training)
3. Higher deal size (upsell enterprise)
4. Shorter cycle (streamline pilot)
```
