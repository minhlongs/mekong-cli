FAQ

What is an MCU?
1 MCU = 1 command execution credit. Simple commands cost 1 MCU, complex multi-step workflows cost 3-5 MCU. Credits are deducted only after successful delivery.
Can I use my own LLM?
Yes. Mekong supports any OpenAI-compatible endpoint: Ollama (local), OpenRouter, DashScope, DeepSeek, Anthropic, Google. Set 3 env vars and go.
What payment methods?
All payments processed via Polar.sh — credit card, bank transfer. No PayPal.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from your Polar dashboard. Unused credits expire at end of billing cycle.
